# Firebrand Ad Agent Kit: brief

**Use case.** Firebrand (firebrand.ai) is an AI agent that runs paid ads on 34 platforms (Meta, Google, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Reddit, Snap, Pinterest, Amazon, CTV…) from one canvas. You give it a goal and a budget; it builds the creatives, launches everywhere and moves money hourly toward whatever channel is running hot. It's for lean growth teams at DTC and B2B brands without a media buyer per channel. They need dashboard icons, a landing hero, three feature cards, a launch banner, a live-event invite, a customer thank-you story and an animated hero scene.

**Idea.** *Heat.* Firebrand reads every channel like a thermal camera: spend flows to what's hot. The kit is a magazine cover story about that heat: huge italic serif headlines, folio lines and issue numbers, pull-quote numerals, and a molten thermal gradient (oxblood → orange → amber) that marks the hot channel. Editorial calm, then one white-hot moment.

**Reference.** George Lois's 1960s Esquire covers and 1980s Sports Illustrated typography (one giant word, one image, a folio strip), crossed with FLIR thermal imaging false-colour.

**Palette "Lava"** (dark). `#1A0A06` char (bg) · `#2E120B` ember brown (surface, panels) · `#FFEDE0` ash white (ink, copy) · `#FF4D00` molten orange (accent: signature, hero shapes) · `#FFB000` amber (accent2: heat gradient top, highlights) · `#FF2E63` hot pink (pop: one dot, one "live" tag). Variants borrow Cherry Cola, Ink & Gold, Citrus Pop (light), Desert Dusk and Acid.

**Type "Magazine".** DM Serif Display (`dmserif`), often italic, for hero words: set huge, tight (−0.03em), cropped if it helps. Bricolage Grotesque (`bricolage`) for copy. JetBrains Mono (`mono`) uppercase, tracked 0.3em, for folios, data and chips.

**Shared motif.** The **heat strip**: a thin thermal gradient bar (char → orange → amber → white-hot tip) with mono tick labels, used as a meter, an underline and a divider. Plus the **folio line** (`FIREBRAND · ISSUE 07 · NOV 2026`) on every editorial asset.

**Assets.**
- **Icons** (dashboard UI): Live Campaigns (fire), Agent (robot), Platforms (broadcast), Budget Autopilot (thermometer hot), Creative Studio (magic wand), Audiences (users three), Performance (chart line up), Launch (rocket launch). Solid black default; regular / bold / thin / duotone / lava tile / brutal variants.
- **3D hero "Firebrand Flame"**: the brand mark as an object. A seamless glossy metaball flame rising from a bevelled ember dish with a glowing amber core. Idle: tongues of flame lick upward on a loop, the core breathes, two sparks rise. Hover: the flame grows and brightens. Click: flare-up with a flash. Calm / blazing mood; clay, glossy, metal, glass.
- **Cards**: **Budget Autopilot** (a white thermostat knob, glowing indicator ring that sweeps), **Creative Studio** (a white instant camera with glowing lens), **Launch Everywhere** (a white megaphone with glowing bell rim that pulses out).
- **Banner "Launch Banner"**: cover-style headline "Spend where it's *hot*." with italic accent word; body "One AI agent runs your ads on 34 platforms and moves budget hourly." Hero visual: a "heat index" — ranked platform bars filled with the thermal gradient, the hottest glowing, a huge "34" numeral behind, folio and issue mark. Wide side by side; strip drops body + shows fewer bars; square stacks.
- **Video "Heat Check Live"** (event, 16:9, 10 s, 120 bpm, 5 bars). Bar 1: heat strip draws, folio types, "LIVE WORKSHOP" chip. Bar 2 downbeat: "Heat Check" sweeps in huge italic with a flash; subtitle words. Bar 3: speaker card (photo optional, duotone-heated; fallback monogram) slides in; three agenda lines tick in on beats. Bar 4 downbeat: date block "DEC 3 · 11:00 ET" slams with shock ring. Bar 5: CTA pill pops, glint, embers, fade out.
- **Video "Customer Thank You"** (thank-you, 9:16, 8 s, 4 bars). Bar 1: embers rise, eyebrow "From all of us at Firebrand". Bar 2 downbeat: "Thank you," sweeps in giant italic, then the customer name (logo optional). Bar 3: counter "12,480 campaigns launched together" ticks up with the heat strip filling. Bar 4: signature line, CTA "Your 2027 kickoff call is on us", ember burst, hold, fade.
- **Scene "Agent Console"** (landing hero, 6 s loop). Archetype: **interface as stage**, because Firebrand's promise is one canvas where the agent works for you. A giant isometric tablet lies on a dark plinth; on its screen a heat-index dashboard of bars and a dial. Ad cards rise from the screen like embers to floating platform tiles around it; the dial needle sweeps; tile LEDs flicker; a coffee cup steams on the plinth. Moment: "rebalance" — the hottest bar spikes, a ring flash and a pink LIVE tag pops. Variants: dusk, daylight, cherry, acid.
