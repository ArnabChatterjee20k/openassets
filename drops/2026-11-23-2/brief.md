# Sparkcrate Micro-Grid Kit — brief

**Use case.** Sparkcrate ships a solar + battery micro-grid in a single crate: two people unfold it in one shift, with no crane and no electrician on site. It is sold to mines, farms, clinics, film sets and disaster-relief teams who run on diesel today. They need UI icons for the fleet dashboard (GridOS), a hero object for the landing page, a social banner, a promo video (diesel-swap lease offer), a customer quote video, stickers for posts and decks, and an isometric hero scene.

**Idea.** "Power in a box, and loud about it." Flat brights and hard black outlines, like stencilled shipping crates, safety signage and hazard tape. Every element sits in a black-outlined box with a 2-step hard offset shadow, as though it were stamped onto a pallet label.

**Reference.** Neo-brutalist web (Gumroad 2021 and indie app landers) crossed with industrial stencil signage: ISO shipping-container markings and Otl Aicher's pictogram clarity.

**Palette "Neo-brutalist"** (tuned for industry): `#FFFDF2` cream paper (bg) · `#FFE45C` sun yellow (surface, the solar energy) · `#000000` ink (outlines, shadows, all small text; a deliberate brutalist statement) · `#FF6B4A` hi-vis coral (accent, warmed from #FF5C8A for a safety-vest feel) · `#5C8AFF` panel blue (accent2, solar cells) · `#00D1A0` charge green (pop: battery level, "ON" dots). Variants borrow Blueprint, Lava, Citrus Pop and Bauhaus.

**Type "Poster shout".** Anton, caps, tight leading (0.88) for hero numbers and headlines ("POWER BY 3PM."). Familjen Grotesk for copy and subheads. JetBrains Mono for spec labels (`480 KWH · LFP`), uppercase and wide-tracked, set on yellow tags.

**Shared motif.** The **crate stencil tag**: a black-outlined label with a hard offset shadow and a hazard-stripe edge, carrying a spec line. Charge green appears only as "power on" dots and battery fill.

**Assets.**
- Icons (GridOS dashboard): solar array, battery charging, crate shipping, grid connect, load gauge, crew setup, site pin, outage alert. Solid black by default; regular, bold, duotone, tile and brutal variants.
- Stickers: Hand Arrow, Scribble Circle, Power Burst (bolt in a burst), Solar Panel, Battery Full, "Sparky", an original crate mascot with a solar-panel hat.
- 3D hero "Sparkcrate Unit": a chunky rounded crate with folded solar wings on top, a battery gauge window and a status LED. Idle: the wings breathe open a little and the LED blinks. Hover opens the wings wider; a click pops them open and flashes the gauge. Materials: clay, glossy, metal, glass.
- 3D cards: **Fold Array** (a tilted solar panel with a glowing cell strip), **Crate Battery** (a battery cell with a glowing charge band), **GridOS** (a controller puck with a glowing dial ring).
- Banner "Deploy Day": headline "POWER BY 3PM." with body copy and a CTA. Hero visual: a brutalist card holding an unfolded crate drawing, stencil spec tags (480 kWh · 2 crew · 0 cranes) and a day timeline (8am crate → 3pm power). Side by side when wide, the strip drops the body, stacked when square.
- Video "Diesel Swap Promo" (promo, 1080×1920, 10 s, 120 BPM): b1 hazard tape wipes in; DIESEL $0.42/kWh slams in on beat 2, then a strike-through on beat 3 · b2 a crate drops in with a shake, panels unfold, and SPARKCRATE $0.11/kWh pops · b3 offer card "90 DAYS FREE" + "from $3,900/mo" with an optional site photo · b4 CTA button slams in, stencil tag, hold with a running ticker, exit wipe.
- Video "Customer Quote" (quote, 1080×1080, 8 s): b1 giant quote mark stamps in with an offset shadow · beats 2–5 quote words rise and a coral highlighter swipes across the key phrase · b3 attribution card slides in (photo or initials, name, role, site) · b4 stat tag "−71% FUEL SPEND" pops with a burst, then the tag ticker runs and the frame exits.
- Scene "Crate to Grid" (landing hero, process machine: a crate *becomes* a power plant). On an isometric site pad, a crate stands open with solar rows fanned out of it; cable dots flow into a battery cabinet, then to a cold-storage container and a water pump. Loops: sun rays rotate, current dots travel, battery bars fill, the container fan spins, clouds drift, the pump handle nods. Moment: a "POWER ON" flash as the site lights pop on.
