# Mimo Ad Agent Kit — brief

**Use case.** Mimo is a chat-first AI ad agent: you tell it what you're selling in plain words, and it builds, launches and babysits campaigns on 36 ad platforms (Google, Meta, TikTok, Reddit, LinkedIn, CTV…). It's for small growth teams and solo founders who can't staff a media buyer per channel. Mimo's team needs dashboard icons, a landing-page hero, feature cards, a launch banner, a live-demo event promo and a thank-you post for early users.

**Idea.** "The ad agent you keep in your pocket." Mimo is styled like a 2001 handheld virtual pet: a pearly chrome egg with a tiny LCD face that looks after your campaigns so *you* don't have to. Glossy Aqua buttons, bubble chat windows, chrome blobs, a friendly rounded voice.

**Reference.** Y2K consumer tech: iMac G3 / iBook translucent plastics, Mac OS X Aqua (2000) gel buttons, MSN Messenger chat windows, Tamagotchi packaging; Designers Republic chrome type softened by Fredoka.

**Palette "Chrome Y2K" (light).** `#DADDE3` brushed silver (bg) · `#C3C8D1` chrome panel (surface) · `#101219` ink (all small text) · `#3D5AFE` Aqua blue (accent: buttons, display type, LCD glow) · `#B8C0CC` steel (accent2: rims, grids) · `#FF3DB4` bubblegum pink (pop: one sparkle, one dot, the notification). Chrome gradients run white → `#C3C8D1` → `#8E95A3`. Variants borrow Lilac Haze, Candy, Nordic Frost, Acid and Ultraviolet Club.

**Type "Friendly app".** Fredoka 600 for heroes (tight, -0.02em); Outfit for copy and UI; Caveat for handwritten asides ("psst, it's free"), signatures and little arrows.

**Shared motif.** The *ping bubble*: a glossy pill-shaped chat bubble with a four-point Y2K sparkle in pink. It appears as Mimo's speech, the notification on the hero, the CTA shape and the scene's flying messages.

**Assets.**
- Icons (dashboard nav): Prompt (chat teardrop), Channels (broadcast), Budget (wallet), Audiences (users-three), Creatives (magic wand), Insights (chart-pie), Approvals (seal-check), Alerts (bell-ringing). Solid black default; Aqua tile, chrome bubble, duotone, brutal variants.
- 3D hero "Mimo Pod": pearl/chrome egg handheld, recessed LCD screen with a pixel face, three gel buttons, keyring loop and a pink notification bubble. Idle: face blinks, bubble bobs, LCD breathes; hover: face looks up and glows; click: squash + bubble pops. Materials pearl / chrome / translucent / glossy.
- Cards: "Chat Briefs" (speech bubble with glowing dots), "Channel Sync" (satellite dish with glowing feed), "Spend Guard" (piggy bank with glowing coin slot).
- Banner "36 Platforms Launch": headline "One chat. 36 ad platforms." beside a chrome Aqua chat window: user prompt bubble, Mimo's reply, platform chips launching out; floating chrome sparkles. Strip drops body; square stacks.
- Scene "Prompt to Platforms" — **Interface as stage**, because Mimo's product *is* the chat window: a giant isometric chrome handheld lies on a silver plinth; typed prompt bubbles rise from its screen, become little ad cards and slide along rails to six platform kiosks (search, social, video, CTV…), which light up. Loops: typing dots, spinning sync ring, kiosk screens flicker, sparkles twinkle, a coin counter ticks; moment: a "Launched ✓" pink burst.
- Video "Live Demo Day" (event, 1920×1080, 10 s, 120 BPM): b1 chrome bubble inflates, "MIMO LIVE" eyebrow; b3 hit — title "Launch on 36 platforms in one chat" sweeps in; bar 2 date/time chips pop on beats (Thu, Nov 20 · 11:00 AM PT), host card (image) slides in; bar 3 platform chips orbit, glint; bar 4 "Save your seat → mimo.ad/live" CTA lands, hold, exit.
- Video "Thank You, Early Crew" (thank-you, 1080×1080, 8 s): b1 pod face winks; b2 "Thank you" pops in huge with sparkle burst; bar 2 stat "12,480 campaigns launched" counts up; bar 3 handwritten note + founder signature (Caveat) + optional team photo in a chrome frame; confetti bubbles drift; end on logo line.
