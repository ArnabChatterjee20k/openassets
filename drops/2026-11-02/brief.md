# particles-io — brief

**Use case.** particles.io is a free, no-download, realtime multiplayer 2D shooter: up to 64 players per arena pilot tiny arrow ships, shoot bursts of particles and climb a live leaderboard. **Season 3 "Overclock"** goes live **Fri 14 Nov 2026, 18:00 UTC** (new map "Reactor", ranked squads, 60-tick servers). The team needs in-game HUD icons, a 3D ship for the landing hero and store page, the season banner, a streamer/caster lower-third, the season launch reel, and an animated landing-page scene.

**Idea.** *Juice on paper.* A shooter that looks like a printed arcade instruction card: cream paper, flat brights, every shape boxed in a hard black outline with an offset black shadow. Motion is pure game feel: hit-stop, screen shake, squash, and particles that burst as flat outlined dots.

**Reference.** Neo-brutalist web UI (Gumroad's 2021 redesign, hard shadows and loud flats) crossed with 1980s Japanese arcade cabinet instruction cards and their chunky katakana-style display type.

**Palette "Neo-brutalist" (light).** `#FFFDF2` paper (bg, 60%) · `#FFE45C` arcade yellow (surface: cards, panels, 30%) · `#000000` ink (outlines, shadows, all small text: a deliberate brutalist pure black) · `#FF5C8A` hot pink (accent: player ship, the hit, CTAs) · `#5C8AFF` laser blue (accent2: enemy team, secondary panels) · `#00D1A0` mint (pop: the LIVE dot, one score number, health pickups). Variants borrow Acid (night arena), Ultraviolet Club, Risograph, Citrus Pop.

**Type "Streetwear".** Dela Gothic One for hero words and numerals (PARTICLES.IO, OVERCLOCK, 14.11), tight leading. Familjen Grotesk for copy. Chakra Petch (uppercase, wide-tracked, italic for speed) for HUD labels, stats, chips and timestamps.

**Shared motif.** The **particle trio**: three black-outlined dots in pink / blue / mint, plus the **arrow ship** (a notched triangle). Every container gets a 4–8 px black outline and a hard offset shadow (down-right), never a blur shadow.

**Assets.**
- **Icons** (in-game HUD): Crosshair (Aim), Heart (Health), Shield (Shield), Lightning (Boost), Skull (Eliminations), Trophy (Leaderboard), UsersThree (Squad), WifiHigh (Ping). Solid black default; regular / bold / thin / duotone / arcade tile / brutal sticker variants.
- **3D "Arrow Ship"** (landing hero, store art): a chunky beveled arrowhead ship with a glowing core cockpit, twin thruster pods and three orbiting particle orbs. Idle: orbs circle, thrusters pulse, the core blinks. Hover: the thruster glow and orbit speed rise. Click: the ship recoils and the core flashes like a shot. Calm / hyped mood; clay / glossy / metal / glass.
- **Banner "Season Launch Banner"** (all sizes): eyebrow "Season 3 · Live Fri 14 Nov", headline "OVERCLOCK", body, CTA "Play free at particles.io". Hero: a brutalist game window (title bar + arena) with ships trading particle shots, a leaderboard card and a "64 players / arena" sticker. Strip drops body + leaderboard; square stacks the window above copy.
- **Video "Caster Lower Third"** (lower-third, 1920×1080, 8 s, 120 BPM). Beat 1: a pink slab slams in from the left with a shake; beat 2: avatar card pops (optional image, else initials) and the name "NOVA_K" sweeps; beat 3: role line words "Team Prism · #1 EU Ranked" rise; beat 4: three stat chips (K/D 4.2 · 1,284 elims · Rank #1) pop on beats; bar 3 a particle burst + LIVE dot pulse, glint; bar 4 exits out in reverse stagger. Background is optional gameplay footage still (image), otherwise a dotted arena stand-in.
- **Video "Season 3 Launch"** (launch, 1080×1920 story, 10 s). Bar 1: particles swarm and lock into the arrow ship on beats. Bar 2 downbeat: the ship fires, hit-stop flash, shake, "OVERCLOCK" slams in as stacked outline blocks. Bar 3: three feature cards land on beats (New map: Reactor · Ranked squads · 60-tick servers), optional gameplay screenshot in a window frame. Bar 4: date "FRI 14.11 · 18:00 UTC" + CTA pill "Play free at particles.io" pops, glint. Bar 5: hold with drifting particles, exit.
- **Scene "Arena Live"**. Archetype: **character / mascot**, because a game's heroes are its players: the little arrow ships doing the product's job (dogfighting live) in their own world. Earlier kits used place, interface-as-stage, network and process machine. A floating isometric arena slab on paper, cover blocks with hard outlines, a respawn pad, a server pylon pulsing sync rings, a scoreboard billboard. Loops: four ships circle and strafe, particle shots fly, the pad pulses, the pylon blinks, pickups bob, clouds of dots drift. Moment: a ship is hit and pops into a burst of outlined particles with a "+100". Variants: default / night (Acid) / ultraviolet / riso / citrus.
