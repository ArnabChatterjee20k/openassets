# Formwork Ad Agent Kit: brief

**Use case.** Formwork (formwork.ai) is an AI agent that takes one campaign brief and runs it across 34 ad platforms (Meta, Google, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Reddit, Pinterest, Snap, Amazon, Spotify, X, CTV…). It drafts the creative variants for each placement, launches them, A/B tests them and moves budget every hour. It's for lean growth teams at DTC and B2B brands who can't staff a specialist per channel. They need dashboard icons, a landing-page hero, three feature cards, a launch banner, a promo film for the Founding plan, a customer-quote post and an animated landing-page scene.

**Idea.** Formwork is the mould concrete is cast in: one form, many castings. The agent treats your brief like a Bauhaus workshop treats a material, as a single module (circle, square, triangle) that gets recomposed onto a strict grid of 34 placements. The look is a calm, expensive modernist poster: cream stock, primary shapes in flat overprint, hairline rules and an elegant italic serif doing the talking.

**Reference.** Kandinsky's 1923 Bauhaus form-and-colour questionnaire (yellow triangle, red square, blue circle), Herbert Bayer's exhibition posters, and the calm of a Phaidon monograph cover or a fashion-house lookbook.

**Palette "Bauhaus" (light).** `#F2EBDD` cream stock (bg) · `#E4D9C4` oat board (surface: panels, cards, tiles) · `#111111` press black (ink: all small text, hairlines, hard offset shadows) · `#D9381E` vermilion (accent: the circle, CTAs, big flat poster shapes) · `#1D4E9E` cobalt (accent2: the square, data, secondary fields) · `#F2B90F` chrome yellow (pop: the triangle, one spark per asset). Variants borrow Swiss, Ink & Gold, Nordic Frost, Terracotta and Risograph.

**Type "Editorial luxe".** Instrument Serif for heroes, set large with the italic for one key word ("Brief once, *run everywhere*"); Familjen Grotesk for copy, chips and buttons; Space Mono for eyebrows, platform labels, numbers ("Nº 34"), uppercase with 0.3em tracking.

**Motif.** The three primaries as the agent's mark (red circle, blue square, yellow triangle) sitting on a hairline grid of placement tiles. In every asset one module moves from the "brief" onto the grid and multiplies. Hairline registration crosses and "Nº" numbering give the printed-matter feel.

**Assets.**
- Icons (dashboard nav): brief (PencilRuler), creative variants (Shapes), channels (SquaresFour), budget split (ChartPieSlice), A/B test (ArrowsSplit), audiences (UsersThree), autopilot (SteeringWheel), performance (ChartLineUp). Solid black default; regular/bold/thin/duotone/tile/poster variants.
- 3D hero "Campaign Engine" (landing page): a kinetic Bauhaus totem: cream rounded plinth, cobalt rounded cube, vermilion sphere balanced on top, a yellow triangular prism leaning on the cube, and a thin black orbit ring carrying small channel beads. Idle: the ring orbits, beads pulse in sequence, the sphere settles. Hover: orbit speeds up. Click: sphere hops and beads flash. Materials clay/glossy/metal/glass, calm/live mood.
- Cards: "Autopilot" (hourly budget moves → a white compass with a glowing needle), "Ad Studio" (creative generation → a white megaphone with a glowing bell ring), "Audience Finder" (targeting → a white magnifying glass with a glowing lens).
- Banner "34 Channels Launch" (all sizes): headline left in Instrument italic; hero right: a Bauhaus poster grid of platform tiles in oat, a big vermilion circle overlapping, a cobalt square, a yellow triangle, Space Mono platform labels, a "live" counter chip. Stacks on square; strip drops body.
- Scene "Brief Foundry" (landing hero, 6 s loop). Archetype: **process machine**, because Formwork takes one input and casts it into many outputs (the last kit's scene was an interface-as-stage desk). A brief sheet drops into a hopper on a cream workshop floor; primary-colour gears turn; a stamping press thumps; ad tiles (circle, square, triangle) ride a conveyor out to a rack of channel screens that light up. Moment: the press stamps and all screens flash on. Variants: day, dusk, night shift, Swiss, terracotta.
- Video "Founding Plan Promo" (promo, 16:9, 8 s, 120 BPM): bar 1: grid lines draw, three primaries drop onto the grid on beats 1-2-3. Downbeat bar 2: the circle swells into a big red field, the offer "First month free" sweeps in (Instrument italic), price "$149/mo" and strike-through on "$249". Bar 3: three feature chips pop on beats, the 34 tiles fill. Bar 4: CTA "Claim by Nov 30 → formwork.ai", glint, shapes exit. Optional product screenshot in a framed card.
- Video "Customer Quote" (quote, 1:1, 9 s, 120 BPM): oat card on cream, an oversized vermilion quote mark circle rolls in, the quote words rise line by line (Instrument), a key phrase underlines in yellow on beat; then the headshot cameo (optional image, falls back to initials in a cobalt square), name, role and company; a stat chip "−38% CPA in 6 weeks" pops; hold with drifting shapes, exit.
