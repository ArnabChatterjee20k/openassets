# Folio Ad Agent Kit: brief

**Use case.** Folio is an AI ad agent for growth teams and agencies. Connect 32 ad platforms once (Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Amazon, Reddit, Pinterest, Snap, CTV…) and the agent plans, launches, bids and re-allocates spend across all of them from one canvas, reporting in plain language. The team needs dashboard icons, a landing-page hero object, feature cards, a launch banner, a milestone post, a product intro film and an animated hero scene.

**Idea.** Folio treats your ad spend like the front page of a financial daily: 32 platforms set in a strict 8 × 4 grid of columns, and the agent is a single red square that moves across the page, deciding where the money goes. Everything is paper, ink and hairline rules; red marks only the agent's decision.

**Reference.** International Typographic Style (Josef Müller-Brockmann's grid posters, Wim Crouwel's numbered systems) set in the voice of a luxury weekend supplement: huge Instrument Serif italics over mono data tables (the *FT Weekend* / *Kinfolk* register).

**Palette "Swiss" (light).** `#F5F5F0` newsprint (bg) · `#E6E6E1` stone (surface: cells, cards) · `#0A0A0A` ink (type, rules, outlines) · `#E3000F` Swiss red (accent: the agent square, the one decision, large display shapes) · `#8C8C88` graphite (accent2: inactive cells, captions, gridlines) · pop = red, in a single dot. Variants borrow Ink & Gold (night edition), Nordic Frost, Bauhaus, Blueprint and Risograph.

**Type "Editorial luxe".** Instrument Serif (italic) for hero words and big numbers ("One agent.", "$10M"). Familjen Grotesk for copy and subheads. Space Mono uppercase, tracked 0.24em, for labels, cell numbers and data ("PLATFORM 07 / META", "ROAS 4.2×").

**Shared motif. The red square on the grid.** Every asset carries a numbered grid (hairline rules, 01–32 cell indices in mono) and one red square: the agent's current move. Captions sit in mono under a 1 px rule, like a newspaper table.

**Assets.**
- **Icons** (dashboard nav): GridNine (platforms), Sparkle (agent), Wallet (budget), ChartBar (reports), Target (audiences), Megaphone (campaigns), ArrowsSplit (A/B tests), ClockCountdown (pacing). Solid black default; variants regular / bold / thin / duotone / red tile / ink brutal.
- **3D hero "Platform Grid"** (landing page): a stone tray holding a 4 × 4 grid of white rounded tiles (the platforms) and one glossy red tile, the agent. Idle: the red tile hops cell to cell on a loop and tiles it lands near dip and ripple; one tile at a time lifts like a bar chart. Hover lifts all tiles into a chart; click sends a ripple from the red tile. Material: clay / glossy / metal / glass; calm / busy mood.
- **Cards**: "Autobid" (a white rotary dial with a glowing indicator ring that sweeps), "Budget Guard" (a white padlock with a glowing keyhole that blinks then locks), "Reports" (three rounded bars on a plinth, the tallest topped with a glowing cap that rises).
- **Banner "32 Platforms Launch"**: "32 platforms. *One* agent." A hero 8 × 4 grid of platform cells (index, name, live spend) with the red agent square sitting on the winning cell, a hairline arrow from the agent to it, and a mono "Reallocated +$4,120 → TikTok" ticket. Copy column left; stacks on square; strip drops body copy.
- **Video "Spend Milestone"** (milestone, 1080×1080, 8 s, 120 BPM, 4 bars). Bar 1: grid rules draw in on beats, cells fill one by one. Bar 2 downbeat: red square snaps to centre, "$10,000,000" counts up in Instrument italic. Bar 3: "managed by Folio agents" words in; three mono stat rows tick in one per beat (32 platforms · 1,400 teams · 4.2× ROAS). Bar 4: thank-you line + optional logo, glint, red square blinks, fade.
- **Video "Meet Folio"** (intro, 1920×1080, 10 s, 120 BPM, 5 bars). Bar 1: a single red square on paper; the grid unfolds from it (rules sweep out). Bar 2: platform names type into cells as the square visits them on beats. Bar 3 downbeat: grid slides left, "Meet *Folio.*" sweeps in. Bar 4: three feature lines one per beat (Plans. Launches. Rebalances.) with optional product screenshot in a hairline frame. Bar 5: CTA "Start free at folio.ai" + mono tag, glint, exit.
- **Scene "Allocation Desk"** (6 s loop). Archetype: **network / system**, because Folio connects one agent to 32 platforms and moves money between them (Multiball used interface-as-stage and process machine). An isometric white plinth with an engraved grid; at the centre a red agent cube on a pedestal with a rotating ring; around it platform pillars (bar-chart blocks, labelled) joined by ground rails. Red packets (budget) travel the rails; pillar tops blink; a ticker tape scrolls on the console. Moment: one pillar wins: red flash, it grows, and a "+$4,120" chip pops. Variants: night edition, blueprint, bauhaus, frost.
