# Junction Ad Agent Kit — brief

**Use case.** Junction is an AI ad agent for lean growth teams: you file one brief and it plans, launches, paces and reports campaigns across 32 ad platforms (Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Reddit, Pinterest, Snap, CTV, audio, retail media…). Its team needs dashboard icons, a landing-page hero, feature cards, a launch banner, a "$100M dispatched" milestone story and a channel intro for their product videos.

**Idea.** "One brief. 32 lines." Junction is a transit authority for ads: every platform is a station, and each campaign is a coloured line leaving the central interchange on time. Wayfinding signage, departure boards, line bullets and route maps, set in summer-fresh juice colours instead of grey municipal ones.

**Reference.** Massimo Vignelli and Unimark's 1970s transit signage and diagrammatic subway map (45° lines, round station dots, black sign bands with white type), crossed with the bright enamel of 1960s Swiss rail posters and split-flap departure boards.

**Palette "Citrus Pop" (light).** `#FFF8E1` cream (bg) · `#FFE9A8` lemon panel (surface) · `#1C2B1A` deep olive ink (all small text, sign bands) · `#FF7A00` orange line (accent: line 1, hero type, CTA) · `#2BB24C` lime line (accent2: line 2, live status) · `#FF3D7F` grapefruit (pop: one station dot, one notification). Variants borrow Night Line (Forest Moss), Swiss, Bauhaus, Sunset Sorbet and Blueprint.

**Type "Civic signage".** Big Shoulders Display 900 in caps for hero words and station names (tight leading 0.86, slight negative tracking); Outfit for product copy; JetBrains Mono, uppercase and wide-tracked, for line codes, times and data ("L07 · META · 09:41").

**Shared motif.** The *line bullet*: a round station dot with a white core, joined by thick rounded lines that turn at 45°. It appears as the route map on the banner, the lamps on the 3D signal, the beam on the intro, the dots on the scene's tracks and the progress strip on the milestone.

**Assets.**
- Icons (dashboard nav): Brief (clipboard-text), Routes (path), Dispatch (train-simple), Budget (coins), Audiences (users-three), Timetable (calendar-dots), Performance (chart-line-up), Alerts (siren). Solid black default; regular / bold / thin / duotone / sign tile (olive band, orange glyph) / enamel brutal variants.
- 3D hero "Junction Signal": a chunky rounded transit signal head on a short post, three glowing lenses under visors (orange = planning, lime = live, pink = needs you). Idle: lamps step through the sequence, live lamp breathes; hover: all lenses warm up; click: squash + every lamp flashes. Materials enamel / glossy / chrome / clay.
- Cards: "Route Planner" (a signpost with a glowing arrow blade), "Spend Pacer" (a stopwatch with a glowing bezel ring), "Creative Lab" (a megaphone with a glowing rim).
- Banner "32 Lines Launch": headline "ONE BRIEF. 32 LINES." beside a Vignelli-style route map: three coloured lines leave a JUNCTION interchange at 45° to station dots labelled with platforms, plus a departure-board chip ("L07 META · ON TIME"). Strip drops body and some stations; square stacks map above copy.
- Scene "Junction Interchange". Archetype **network / system**, because Junction connects one brief to many platforms (yesterday's kit used interface-as-stage). An isometric elevated interchange on a cream plaza: a central station hall with a departure board and a JUNCTION sign band, three raised tracks (orange, lime, pink) curving out to six platform stations (search, social, video, CTV, audio, retail) with little canopies. Loops: trains glide along each line, departure board flips, station lamps flicker, clock hand turns, clouds and a balloon drift. Moment: "DISPATCH": the hub flashes and all stations light up in sequence. Variants: day / dusk / night line / swiss / blueprint.
- Video "Milestone Board" (milestone, 1080×1920, 10 s, 120 BPM): b1 olive sign band drops in with "JUNCTION · MILESTONE"; b2–b4 split-flap digits flip one by one to "$100,000,000"; bar 2 hit — flash, line bullets burst outward, label "AD SPEND DISPATCHED"; bar 3 stat rows ("32 platforms · 18,402 campaigns · 0 missed launches") tick in like a timetable; optional team photo in a station frame; bar 4 "Thank you, riders →" CTA, hold with lines running, exit.
- Video "Line Intro" (intro, 1920×1080, 6 s): b1 three coloured lines draw across the frame at 45° and meet at centre; b2 hit — the interchange dot pops, shock ring, logo (image) or wordmark slams into a black sign band; b3 tagline "Your ad agent on 32 platforms" words in, line codes scroll in mono; bar 2 glint, chips; exit by lines pulling away.
