# Latch Lid-Commit Kit — brief

**Use case.** Latch is a macOS menu-bar utility for developers: when you close your laptop lid, it stages your work, writes a `wip:` commit on a safe branch and pushes it before the machine sleeps. No more "I lost a day because my battery died". The team needs: UI icons for the menu-bar app and settings, a 3D hero for the landing page, light-beam cards for its three features, a launch-week banner, a "Latch 2.0" announcement film, a Product Hunt countdown story, a hand-drawn doodle pack and an animated hero illustration.

**Idea.** The laptop as an engineering drawing. Every asset is a cyanotype blueprint: white drafting lines on deep Prussian blue, dimension arrows, a hinge drawn in side elevation, title blocks in the corner. Over it sits magazine-cover type: a big DM Serif headline set like a *Wired* cover. The one bright thing is the yellow commit dot that appears when the lid shuts.

**Reference.** Anna Atkins' cyanotypes and 1950s mechanical drawings (title blocks, section hatching, dimension lines), with 1990s *Wired* and *Esquire* cover typography on top.

**Palette "Blueprint" (dark).** `#0F3B8C` cyanotype blue (bg) · `#134AA8` paper fold / panels (surface) · `#EAF2FF` drafting white (ink) · `#FFFFFF` chalk line (accent, for linework and hero type) · `#7FB2FF` pencil-blue construction lines (accent2) · `#FFDD00` commit dot (pop: only the commit and the CTA). Variants borrow Swiss, Ink & Gold, Nordic Frost and Acid.

**Type "Magazine".** DM Serif Display for heroes (italic for the one emphasised word: *committed*). Bricolage Grotesque for copy. JetBrains Mono for labels, hashes, branch names and dimension callouts (`110°`, `a3f9c21`).

**Shared motif: the hinge arc.** A drafted dimension arc that sweeps from 110° (open) to 0° (shut) and ends in the yellow commit dot. It's the dial in the countdown, the callout in the banner, the moment in the films and the scene.

**Assets.**
- **Icons (8, menu bar + settings):** laptop, git-commit, git-branch, cloud-arrow-up (push), moon (sleep trigger), clock-counter-clockwise (restore), wifi-slash (offline queue), shield-check (nothing lost). Solid black by default; regular/bold/thin/duotone, a blueprint tile and a brutal variant.
- **Stickers (6):** Close Arrow (an arc arrow showing the lid closing), Scribble Circle, Sparkles, Commit Graph (a branch doodle with yellow commit dots), Moon & Zzz, and the mascot "Clam", a sleepy laptop whose screen face has closed eyes.
- **3D hero "Lid-Close Laptop":** a rounded clamshell laptop with a glowing commit LED on its front lip. Idle: the lid dips toward shut and springs back while the LED pulses once. Hover opens the lid wider and brightens the screen; a click snaps the lid half-shut and flashes the LED. Material clay/glossy/metal/glass, mood calm/lively.
- **Cards (3):** *Lid Trigger*: a crescent moon with a glowing star. *Auto Commit*: a floppy disk with a glowing label stripe. *Branch Guard*: a shield with a glowing keyhole.
- **Banner "Launch Week Hero":** headline left ("Close the lid. It's *committed*."), and on the right a blueprint side elevation of a laptop with the hinge arc dimensioned 110° → 0°, the yellow commit dot, and mono commit chips (`a3f9c21 · wip: checkout flow`). Stacks on square, drops the chips on the strip.
- **Video "Latch 2.0 Announcement" (announcement, 1920×1080, 8 s, 120 BPM):** bar 1, the drafting grid fades up and construction lines draw a laptop in side elevation. Downbeat of bar 2, the lid swings shut along the hinge arc, the commit dot hits with a flash and ring, and the hash types out. Bar 3, the eyebrow "Announcing Latch 2.0" and the DM Serif headline sweep in, then the feature words. Bar 4, the CTA pill pops on the beat (`brew install latch`), a glint, and the frame fades out. An optional screenshot slides into a framed panel.
- **Video "Launch Countdown" (countdown, 1080×1920, 10 s):** a Product Hunt launch story. A hinge dial counts 5→1, one digit per beat-pair, and the lid arc closes 110°→0° step by step with tick marks. At 0 the dot turns yellow, there's a flash, and "We're live." sweeps in with the date, time and CTA. An optional logo goes in the title block.
- **Scene "Lid to Origin":** a *process machine*, because Latch turns one physical act (shutting the lid) into a pushed commit. An isometric night desk: the laptop's lid closes (the moment), a yellow commit cube shoots up a pneumatic tube to an "origin" server cabinet, whose drawer LEDs blink as it lands. Desk lamp, mug steam, a sticky note, cable, a clock, stars through the window. 8 s loop.
