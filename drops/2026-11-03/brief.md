# E-commerce Flash Sale — brief

**Use case.** Mochi Mode (mochimode.shop), an online store for cute desk tech (keycap sets, mice, headphones, desk mats), runs its **11.11 Flash Sale**: 48 hours, Tue 11 Nov 00:00 to Wed 12 Nov 23:59, up to 60% off, code `FLASH11`, free shipping over $35. The team needs storefront UI icons, a 3D gift box for the landing hero, the sale banner, a post-purchase thank-you story and a "100K orders" milestone film, plus an animated landing-page scene.

**Idea.** *Kawaii mecha.* A pastel candy shop run from a giant-robot cockpit: soft pink and lilac fields, crossed by angular HUD readouts, targeting brackets and countdown timers. Sweet surfaces, precise machine type. The sale feels like a mission launch, yet it still looks like a gift.

**Reference.** 1990s anime mecha interfaces (the *Evangelion* NERV screens: bracketed readouts, chevron stripes, angular numerals) printed in Harajuku *decora* / Sanrio packaging pastels. The Designers Republic's techno labels meet a candy wrapper.

**Palette "Candy" (light).** `#FDF3F8` strawberry milk (bg) · `#F7DDEB` wrapper pink (surface, cards) · `#3A1F4A` grape ink (all text and line work) · `#FF7EB6` bubblegum (accent: prices, the hit, CTAs) · `#8A7BFF` lilac laser (accent2: HUD lines, second light) · `#4FD1C5` mint (pop: a status LED, one "LIVE" dot). Variants borrow Lilac Haze, Sunset Sorbet, Memphis, Neo-brutalist and Ultraviolet Club (the one dark "night drop").

**Type "Mecha HUD".** Chakra Petch 700 (italic for speed) for hero words and big numerals (prices, countdowns, counters). Sora for copy. Major Mono Display for labels, order numbers and data chips (wide-tracked).

**Shared motif.** **HUD target brackets** (four corner ticks) framing the hero of every asset, a **notched price tag** with a punched hole, and **chevron hazard stripes** (`/////`) in lilac. Every asset has one bubblegum "hit".

**Assets.**
- **Icons** (storefront UI): ShoppingCartSimple (Add to Cart), ShoppingBagOpen (Shopping Bag), Tag (Sale Tag), Truck (Free Shipping), Gift (Gift Wrap), Lightning (Flash Deal), SealPercent (Coupon), Timer (Deal Timer). Solid black default; regular / bold / thin / duotone / candy tile / brutal sticker variants.
- **3D "Flash Sale Gift Box"** (landing hero): a chunky rounded gift box with a ribbon cross, a puffy bow and a hanging notched tag. Idle: the lid peeks open and a glow leaks out, the tag swings, the bow breathes. Hover: the lid lifts more and the glow brightens. Click: the lid pops up and spins, then lands. Calm / lively mood; clay / glossy / metal / glass.
- **Banner "Flash Sale Banner"** (all BANNER_SIZES): eyebrow "11.11 flash sale · 48h only", headline "Up to 60% off", body, CTA "Shop the drop · code FLASH11". Hero: a giant tilted price tag "-60%" inside HUD brackets, a countdown readout "47:59:12", a product chip "Mochi Keycap Set $89 → $35.60" and chevron stripes. Strip drops body + product chip; square stacks the tag above the copy.
- **Video "Order Thank You"** (thank-you, 1080×1920 story, 8 s, 120 BPM). Bar 1: HUD brackets snap in on beats, a parcel drops and lands with a squash on beat 4. Bar 2 downbeat: flash, ribbon burst, "Thank you, Maya!" sweeps in. Bar 3: order chip "#MM-24811 · ships in 24h", optional product photo in a bracketed frame, message words rise. Bar 4: coupon ticket "COMEBACK15 · 15% off your next order" pops on the downbeat, glint, exit.
- **Video "100K Orders"** (milestone, 1920×1080, 10 s, 120 BPM). Bar 1: a HUD gauge ring spins up while a counter rolls 0 → 100,000. Bar 2 downbeat: counter locks, flash + shockwave, confetti tags burst, "orders" label hits. Bar 3: three stat chips land on beats (48 hours · 31 countries · 4.9★). Bar 4: thank-you line + optional team photo / logo. Bar 5: brand pill, glint, exit.
- **Scene "Flash Sale Fulfilment"**. Archetype: **process machine**, because a flash sale turns thousands of taps into packed parcels at speed (the last kit used a network). A giant isometric phone shows a pulsing "BUY" button; order capsules pop out into a gachapon-style hopper dome; boxes ride a conveyor through a ribbon-wrapping arch and into a pastel delivery truck. Loops: capsules bob, rollers spin, LEDs blink, the truck idles and puffs, tags drift. Moment: a "SOLD OUT" stamp slams onto a box with a flash. Variants: default / lilac / sorbet dusk / night drop / brutal.
