# Ad Spend Mandate Kit (Mandate): brief

**Use case.** Mandate (mandate.ai) is an AI agent that runs paid ads across 32 platforms (Meta, Google, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Reddit, Snap, Pinterest, Amazon, CTV…). You give it a mandate (a budget, a target CPA, guardrails) and it allocates, bids, rebalances overnight and closes the books every morning. It's for growth leads and CFO-minded founders who treat ad spend like capital. The team needs dashboard icons, a 3D hero for the landing page, three feature cards, a launch banner, a milestone post, a channel intro and an animated hero scene.

**Idea.** Ad spend run like a fund. No glowing robots: the look of a private bank's annual report. It's black paper with gold-foil rules, ledger columns, ticker tape and big blunt numbers. The agent is the portfolio manager; every platform is a position.

**Reference.** Brutalist financial print: 1980s Wall Street annual reports with gold-foil stamping, crossed with Wim Crouwel's grids and the departure-board split-flap ticker. Raw structure, black-tie finish.

**Palette "Ink & Gold" (dark).** `#0D0D0F` ink-black (bg) · `#1C1C20` graphite (surface: ledger panels, the machine body) · `#F3EDE2` bone paper (ink: all text) · `#C9A45C` foil gold (accent: rules, the hero number, CTAs) · `#6E6A62` pewter (accent2: grid lines, secondary data, muted rows) · `#E8D9B5` champagne (pop: one highlight per asset, the ▲ tick, a glint). Variants borrow Swiss (paper + red), Bauhaus, Blueprint, Forest Moss and Cherry Cola.

**Type "Brutalist".** Archivo Black for heroes: huge, tight (-0.04em), often cropped off the edge, numbers set like a headline. Space Mono for copy, and Space Mono uppercase at 0.3em tracking for eyebrows, ledger labels, ticker symbols (`META ▲ 24.1%`).

**Shared motif: the allocation bar + gold rule.** A long horizontal bar sliced into 32 segments of different widths (the portfolio), with one segment lit in foil gold, sitting on a hairline gold rule with tick marks. Data is written like a ledger line with ticker-style symbols.

**Assets.**
- **Icons (8, dashboard UI):** spend (Coins), rebalance (Scales), bids (Crosshair), roas (ChartLineUp), approvals (SealCheck), ledger (Receipt), guardrails (ShieldCheck), networks (Broadcast). Solid black default; regular/bold/thin/duotone/tile (ink tile, gold glyph)/brutal variants.
- **3D icon "Spend Vault":** a chunky bank vault with a combination dial and gold-lit seams. Idle: the dial ticks round, the status lamp blinks, gold light breathes from the door seam; hover cracks the door open and the glow grows; click spins the dial and pops a gold coin out. Materials clay/glossy/metal/glass; calm/active mood.
- **3D cards:** "Rebalancer" (balance scale, glowing pivot gem, pans tip and settle), "Bid Desk" (auction gavel with a glowing band, strikes on click), "Spend Caps" (padlock with a glowing keyhole, shackle clicks).
- **Banner "One Mandate":** left: eyebrow `MANDATE · 32 PLATFORMS`, headline "32 platforms. One mandate.", mono body, CTA "Set your mandate". Right: a ledger board of platform rows with allocation bars, ticker deltas and a big gold "4.2× ROAS". Strip drops body and rows to 3; square stacks the board above the copy.
- **Video "Spend Milestone"** (milestone, 1:1, 8 s @ 60 fps, 120 BPM). Bar 1: gold rule draws across, eyebrow "MILESTONE · NOV 2026", ticker tape starts crawling. Beat 5 (bar 2 downbeat): the counter rolls up with a split-flap feel and lands on "$100M" with a flash and a shockwave rule. Bar 3: "ad spend managed by Mandate agents" words rise; allocation bar fills segment by segment. Bar 4: "Thank you, 4,200 teams." plus logo (image), glint on the number, fade.
- **Video "Channel Intro"** (intro, 16:9, 6 s). Beats 1–3: the 32-segment allocation bar slams in segment by segment on 16ths, platform tickers flicker across. Bar 2 downbeat: the bar collapses into a gold rule, the wordmark "MANDATE" slams in huge and cropped with a shake; episode label "EP. 14 · THE MORNING LEDGER" types in. Bar 3: glint, tagline, exit wipe. Optional host photo/logo in a framed plate.
- **Scene "Allocation Engine"** (archetype **process machine**: Mandate takes one budget and sorts it, coin by coin, into the platforms that earn. The last two kits used vehicle/journey and network). An isometric coin-sorting machine in a dark vault room: a hopper fed by a falling stream of gold coins, a graphite sorter with gauges and a ticker display, a conveyor, and a row of glass tubes (one per platform) filled to different heights like a bar chart, with brass labels. Loops: coins fall, gears spin, gauge needles wave, ticker scrolls, lamps flicker, tube levels breathe. Moment: a rebalance: the gate swings, a coin diverts to the winning tube, which flashes gold. Variants: vault night / daylight Swiss / blueprint / forest / cherry.
