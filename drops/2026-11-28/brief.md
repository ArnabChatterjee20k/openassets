# Sprue Rapid Molding Kit: brief

**Use case.** Sprue (fictional) makes real injection-molded plastic parts in days instead of months: upload a CAD file, get an instant quote, Sprue cuts the tool on its automated line and ships end-use parts in ABS, PC, PA12 or TPU in 5 days. The audience is hardware engineers and founders who need 500–50,000 production parts without a 10-week tooling wait. They need app icons for the quoting dashboard, a doodle pack for the landing page and decks, a 3D hero, product cards (Instant Quote, Resin Library, Fulfillment), a launch banner, a launch film and a lower third for their factory-tour and founder interviews.

**Idea.** "Fresh off the line." The joy of a just-molded part: glossy lime plastic still warm, popping off its runner like a model-kit piece. Night-shift factory in the woods: deep moss darkness, the lime part as the light source, one orange spark of heat.

**Reference.** 1970s molded-plastic optimism: Tupperware and Fisher-Price print ads, Vignelli-era Heller stacking ware, and the triple "racing stripe" of 70s packaging, all set in juicy Shrikhand.

**Palette "Forest Moss"** (dark): `#0E1A14` night moss (bg) · `#18291F` spruce panel (surface) · `#EAF2DC` paper mint (ink) · `#B5E550` fresh-molded lime (accent: the part, the hit, CTAs) · `#4E8A5B` pine (accent2: runners, stripes, data) · `#FF9F43` melt orange (pop: heat, one dot, one word). Variants borrow Citrus Pop, Mid-century, Cherry Cola, Risograph and Nordic Frost.

**Type "70s sunshine".** Shrikhand for hero words (juicy italic, tight −0.02em, never all caps). Fraunces for copy (600, warm). Outfit 500/800 for labels, prices, chips; uppercase, tracked 0.22em.

**Shared motif.** The **sprue tree** and the **70s triple stripe**. Parts hang on a thin runner frame by little gate stubs; a lime / pine / orange stripe trio sweeps in a rounded arc behind heroes. Prices and lead times sit in pill-shaped "molded" chips with a soft highlight.

**Assets.**
- Icons (quoting dashboard): Upload CAD (FileArrowUp), Instant Quote (Calculator), Lead Time (Timer), Part (Cube), Tolerance (Ruler), Resin (Drop), Production (Factory), Shipment (Package). Solid black default; regular / bold / thin / duotone / tile / molded variants.
- Stickers: Loop Arrow, Scribble Circle, Spark Burst (marks); Molded Gear, Sprue Tree (objects); Pellet Pal (original mascot: a round resin pellet with a face).
- 3D hero "Injection Mold": two rounded mold halves on guide pins with a nozzle on top and a glossy lime gear inside. Idle: the mold clamps shut, glows orange with heat, opens and the gear pops up on ejector pins. Hover opens it wider; click ejects the gear with a spin. Materials clay / glossy / metal / glass; mood steady / rush.
- 3D cards: **Instant Quote** (stopwatch, glowing crown + dial), **Resin Library** (pellet canister with glowing band), **Fulfillment** (parcel box with glowing tape strip).
- Banner "Production Parts Launch": "Real plastic parts in 5 days." Hero visual: a sprue tree of lime parts (gear, knob, clip, ring) in front of a 70s stripe arc, a quote card (`PA12 · 2,500 pcs · $0.42/part`) and chips (`Quote in 60 sec`, `Ships Dec 3`). Wide side by side, strip drops body, square stacks.
- Video "Interview Lower Third" (lower-third, 1920×1080, 8 s, 120 BPM): b1 the triple stripe draws in from the left on the beat, headshot pellet pops (backOut) · beat 3 name sweeps in in Shrikhand, role words rise · b2 a molded lead-time chip snaps on, gloss glint at bar 3 · b4 retracts in reverse on beats. Optional footage still behind; placeholder stage otherwise.
- Video "Sprue Launch" (launch, 1080×1920, 10 s, 120 BPM): b1 "Upload CAD" file card drops, wireframe gear draws itself · b2 quote ticks to `$0.42 / part` on beats, `Quote in 60 sec` · b3 downbeat: mold halves slam, orange heat flash + shockwave, lime gear ejects and spins; "Parts in 5 days." in Shrikhand · b4–5 stripe sweep, CTA pill "Get a quote → sprue.parts" pops, glint, exit. Optional product photo replaces the gear.
- Scene "Night Shift Line": **process machine**, because Sprue turns resin pellets and a CAD file into parts (the last kit used a place). A cutaway workshop at night: a CAD screen beams a gear, a hopper rains pellets into a barrel with a turning screw, the clamp opens and closes, parts drop onto a conveyor into a parcel box; pine forest outside the window. Loops: pellets falling, screw spin, conveyor, screen scanline, heat shimmer, stars. Moment: the mold opens with an orange flash and a lime part pops out. 6 s loop.
