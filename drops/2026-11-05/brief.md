# AI Ad Agent Launch — brief

**Use case.** Mycel (mycel.ai) is an AI agent that plans, launches and rebalances paid campaigns across 32 ad platforms (Meta, Google, TikTok, Reddit, LinkedIn, CTV and more) from one prompt. It is for founders and two-person growth teams who can't staff a media buyer per channel. The team needs app UI icons (dashboard, campaign builder), a 3D agent mascot for the landing page, a launch banner, the "Mycel 2.0 — now on 32 platforms" announcement film, a 9:16 countdown story for their **Mycel Live** launch stream (Thu 20 Nov 2026 · 10:00 PT), three feature cards and an animated landing-page scene.

**Idea.** Mycelium: the hidden network under a night forest that feeds every tree from one root system. Mycel is that root system for ads: one agent, threads of lime light running out to dozens of platforms. The agent talks through a green phosphor terminal, so every asset pairs organic glowing threads with crisp CRT type.

**Reference.** 1980s green-phosphor terminals (VT100, Apple ][ monitors) crossed with bioluminescent forest photography (glowing fungi, foxfire); Ryoji Ikeda's data minimalism for the readouts.

**Palette "Forest Moss" (dark).** `#0E1A14` forest night (bg) · `#18291F` moss bed / terminal glass (surface) · `#EAF2DC` lichen paper (ink) · `#B5E550` foxfire lime (accent: threads, cursor, CTAs) · `#4E8A5B` fern (accent2: secondary threads, grids) · `#FF9F43` ember (pop: the "live" dot, the ROAS number, one spark per asset). Variants borrow Deep Sea, Acid, Blueprint, Matcha (light) and Mid-century (light).

**Type "Terminal".** VT323 for display (big integer sizes, tight but never negative tracking below -0.01em: it's pixel). JetBrains Mono for copy and prompts. Silkscreen caps, wide-tracked, for eyebrows, chips and data labels.

**Shared motif.** **The thread + cursor.** A branching lime hypha line runs from a single block cursor `█` to many nodes. Each asset has one prompt line (`> mycel launch --everywhere`) and one ember "LIVE" dot. Scanlines at very low opacity over hero surfaces.

**Assets.**
- **Icons (8, dashboard UI):** agent (Robot), campaigns (Megaphone), audiences (Target), ROAS (ChartLineUp), budget (Coins), channels (ShareNetwork), clicks (CursorClick), creatives (MagicWand). Solid black default; regular/bold/thin/duotone/tile (moss tile, lime glyph)/brutal variants.
- **3D icon "Mycel Agent":** the mascot: a rounded CRT-terminal head with pixel eyes and a cursor mouth, an antenna whose tip glows lime, and three ad tiles orbiting on a thread ring. Idle: eyes blink, cursor blinks, tiles orbit, antenna pulses; hover speeds the orbit and widens the eyes; click flashes the screen and spins the tiles. Materials clay/glossy/metal/glass; calm/busy mood.
- **3D cards:** "Autopilot Budgets" (coin stack, top coin rim glows), "Creative Studio" (magic wand with glowing star tip), "Audience Targeting" (bullseye with a dart, glowing centre ring).
- **Banner "Launch Everywhere":** headline "One prompt. 32 ad platforms." left; right, a terminal window with the prompt typed, from which lime threads branch to platform chips (Meta, Google, TikTok, Reddit…) and an ember ROAS chip "4.1× ROAS". Strip drops the body; square stacks terminal over copy.
- **Video "Platform Announcement"** (announcement, 16:9, 8 s @ 60 fps, 120 BPM). Bar 1: CRT power-on line opens, prompt types `> mycel launch --everywhere`. Bar 2 downbeat: Enter → flash; threads burst from the cursor and platform chips pop in on each beat while a counter rolls 0→32. Bar 3: chips drift back, title "NOW ON 32 PLATFORMS" sweeps in, sub-copy words in. Bar 4: CTA pill on beat, glint, fade. Optional product screenshot sits in a terminal frame.
- **Video "Launch Countdown"** (countdown, 9:16, 10 s). Beat 1: header "MYCEL LIVE" + date boot in. Then T-5…1, one digit per second, huge VT323 inside a ring of 32 dashes that drain as time runs out; scanline roll on each tick. 6 s hit: flash, "WE'RE LIVE" + thread burst; hold with stream link and host avatar (image) chip; fade.
- **Scene "Agent Network"** — archetype **network / system**, because Mycel's whole job is one agent connected to many platforms (yesterday's kit was a place, the one before a process machine). An isometric moss-and-soil slab at night: the agent's CRT console in the centre, six small platform islands around it (billboard, phone feed, search box, TV, video player, shop tile), joined by glowing hypha threads along which ad packets travel. Mushrooms, ferns, pines, fireflies. Loops: packets travel, console cursor blinks, phone screen scrolls, billboard flickers, fireflies drift, stars twinkle. Moment: "LAUNCH": a ripple runs out from the console and every platform lights up at once. Variants: night / dawn / daylight / deep sea / acid.
