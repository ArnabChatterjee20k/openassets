# Halfstream Managed Kafka — brief

**Use case.** Halfstream runs Apache Kafka in the customer's AWS account and carries the pager. Customers pay AWS on-demand prices for servers and storage plus 25% on brokers, which comes to about half their MSK bill. The buyers are platform and data engineers at Series A–C startups who are tired of MSK invoices and 3 a.m. broker pages. They need console icons, a landing-page hero, product cards for the three pillars (brokers, on-call, billing), a launch banner, a "milestone" social clip and a channel/demo-day intro.

**Idea.** *Cut the bill in half.* A strict Swiss grid on warm paper, black hairline rules, and one red: a red block sliced cleanly down the middle. On top of that rational base sits urban night signage: Bungee set like vertical blade signs, and VT323 readouts like a broker's terminal log ticking away.

**Reference.** Josef Müller-Brockmann's concert posters (grid, rules, one hot colour) crossed with Tokyo and New York vertical shop signs. The International Typographic Style wears a neon street jacket.

**Palette "Swiss" (light).** `#F5F5F0` paper (bg) · `#E6E6E1` card stock (surface) · `#0A0A0A` ink (text, rules, outlines) · `#E3000F` signal red (accent: the half-block, CTAs, the hit) · `#8C8C88` concrete grey (accent2: grid, secondary data) · `#E3000F` pop (one dot, the live LED). Variants borrow Cherry Cola, Blueprint, Neo-brutalist, Nordic Frost and Acid.

**Type "Neon street".** Bungee for hero words, numbers and stacked signage. Outfit for product copy. VT323 for labels, log lines, metrics and eyebrows, set uppercase with wide tracking.

**Shared motif: the half-block and the log.** Every asset carries (1) a red block or bar split at 50% by a hairline (the bill, halved) and (2) a row of small square "message ticks" running along a rule, which is the Kafka log streaming. The half-block always belongs to the price or to the hit; the ticks keep holds alive.

**Assets.**
- **Icons (console UI), 8 Phosphor glyphs:** Brokers (HardDrives), Topic (Queue), Partitions (ArrowsSplit), Consumer Lag (Gauge), On-call Alert (Siren), Cluster Health (Pulse), AWS Bill (Receipt), Savings (PiggyBank). Solid black by default. Variants: regular / bold / thin / duotone / Swiss tile (red squircle, paper glyph) / brutal.
- **Stickers (landing-page doodles), 6:** Pointer Arrow, Scribble Circle, Sparkle Trio (marks); Half-off Burst (a starburst with a down arrow); On-call Pager (a chunky beeper with a red LED); Broker Rack (a little three-unit server with blinking LED dots). Paper fills, red accent, warm-black ink.
- **3D hero "Broker Stack":** three rounded broker blades stacked with gaps, each with a VT-style LED strip. A red "log" of message cubes slides through a groove in the front of each blade. Idle: the cubes cycle, the LEDs ripple and the stack breathes. Hover speeds up the stream; a click pulses the stack and flashes every LED. Materials: clay / glossy / metal / glass. Mood: steady / peak traffic.
- **3D cards (pillars), 3:** *Managed Brokers* (a white server blade with an emissive LED bar), *On-Call Pager* (a white beeper with a glowing screen; it buzzes and its LED blinks), *Usage Billing* (a stack of white coins with an emissive rim, the top coin flipping).
- **Banner "Half the MSK Bill":** the headline "Kafka at half the MSK bill." with a hero bar chart on a Swiss grid. The MSK bar is a full grey column at $18,420/mo; the Halfstream bar is a red column at half height, $9,210/mo, with a dashed 50% line and a stream of message ticks along the baseline. A VT323 chip reads "AWS on-demand + 25% on brokers". It stacks on square and drops the body copy on the strip.
- **Video "Trillion Messages" (milestone, 1080×1080, 8 s, 120 BPM):** bar 1: grid rules draw in, the VT323 eyebrow types, a counter rolls with ticks streaming along the rule. Bar 2 downbeat: the counter lands, a red half-block slams in behind "1T", flash and shake. Bar 3: three stat rows (p99, pages, saved) arrive on beats, then a glint. Bar 4: thank-you line, optional logo, red wipe out.
- **Video "Meet Halfstream" (intro, 1920×1080, 8 s, 120 BPM):** bar 1: an MSK invoice total prints in VT323 ($18,420/mo). Beat 4: a red blade cuts it down the middle, the right half drops away and the number rolls to $9,210. Bar 2 downbeat: HALFSTREAM stacks in like a vertical sign, with a flash. Bar 3: three facts tick in on beats ("AWS on-demand prices", "+25% on brokers", "We carry the pager"). Bar 4: CTA pill and URL, optional logo/screenshot, glint, exit.
- **Scene "Stream Line" (landing-page hero, isometric SMIL, 6 s):** a *process machine*, because Halfstream takes events in, stores them durably and hands them to consumers. Producer hoppers on the left drop red message cubes onto three partition conveyors. The conveyors run through three broker cabinets with blinking LED racks and spinning fans, and consumer arms on the right pick the cubes off. Behind it all is a wall-mounted bill meter whose needle sits at 50%. Moment: one broker's LED turns red and the pager on the post flashes, then it heals back to green.
