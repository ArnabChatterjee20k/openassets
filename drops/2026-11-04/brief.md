# Building Fully Autonomous Factories in America — brief

**Use case.** Redbrick Works (redbrick.works) runs a lights-out CNC machine shop in Dayton, Ohio: upload a STEP file, get an instant quote, and robot-tended 5-axis cells machine your aluminium and steel parts overnight; they ship in 72 hours. Customers are hardware startups and defense/aerospace engineers who need prototypes and short runs made in America, fast. The team needs app/portal UI icons, a 3D hero for the landing page, a quote banner, a tech explainer film, a Thanksgiving greeting for customers and an animated landing-page scene.

**Idea.** *Rust-belt brick at night.* Warm clay and sage factory brick, cut through by orange toolpaths. The factory never sleeps; its signage glows like a diner on Route 4. Heavy signage display type sits on top, and small CRT readouts (G-code, tolerances, lead times) run underneath.

**Reference.** Mid-century American industrial signage (painted brick-wall ads, "MADE IN USA" stencils, WPA works posters) meets the green-screen CNC controller: Bungee letters on clay, VT323 readouts in ink.

**Palette "Terracotta" (light, kept).** `#F1E4D3` plaster (bg) · `#E5CDB2` kiln clay (surface, panels, plates) · `#3B1F14` iron-oxide ink (all text and line work) · `#C8553D` brick red (accent: the hero part, the toolpath, CTAs) · `#4F6D58` machine-enamel sage (accent2: machines, secondary light) · `#F2A541` spindle-spark amber (pop: one LED, the tool tip, sparks). Variants borrow Mid-century, Blueprint (night shift), Bauhaus, Matcha and Desert Dusk.

**Type "Neon street".** Bungee for hero words and big numerals (72H, prices), set tight. Outfit for copy. VT323 for labels, G-code, part numbers and data chips, uppercase and wide-tracked.

**Shared motif.** **The toolpath:** a dashed brick-red line with an amber tool-tip dot that traces each hero (it engraves the greeting, outlines the banner part and draws the scene's part). Every asset also has one small **CRT readout chip** in VT323 (`X 42.180 Y-12.004` / `±0.01 MM`).

**Assets.**
- **Icons** (customer portal UI): Factory (Shop), FileArrowUp (Upload CAD), GearSix (Machining), Robot (Robot Cell), Ruler (Tolerances), SealCheck (QC Passed), Timer (Lead Time), Truck (Shipping). Solid black by default; regular / bold / thin / duotone / clay tile / brutal sticker variants.
- **3D "Robot Cell Arm"** (landing hero): a chunky six-axis industrial robot arm on a round plinth, gripping a machined part, with a status LED ring at the base. Idle: the turret pans, shoulder and elbow lift the part, and the LED breathes. Hover: it lifts higher and the LED brightens. Click: the gripper snaps and the part spins. Calm / busy mood; clay / glossy / metal / glass.
- **Cards** (Redbrick products): "Instant Quote" (price tag with a glowing grommet), "5-Axis Milling" (an end-mill in a tool holder with a glowing collar, spinning), "72h Shipping" (a parcel with a glowing tape band).
- **Banner "Instant Quote Banner"**: eyebrow "Made in Dayton, Ohio", headline "Parts in 72 hours.", CTA "Upload a STEP file". Hero: a machined bracket drawn in isometric with a dashed toolpath, dimension callouts and a quote chip "$148.20 · 25 pcs · 6061-T6". Strip drops body; square stacks.
- **Video "How It Works"** (tech, 1920×1080, 9 s, 120 BPM). Bar 1: a CRT boot line types, the STEP part outlines itself, step 01 "Upload" lands. Bar 2: the toolpath runs, spindle sparks, 02 "Machine" lands on the downbeat with a flash. Bar 3: an inspection scan passes, the "±0.01 mm" chip pops, 03 "Ship" lands, a truck slides. Bar 4: "72H" hits huge, CTA pill pops, glint, exit. Optional part photo replaces the drawn part.
- **Video "Thanksgiving Greeting"** (greeting, 1080×1080, 8 s, 120 BPM). Bar 1: a clay plate slides in; the spindle dot engraves "Happy Thanksgiving" along a toolpath, throwing amber chips. Bar 2 downbeat: the engraving fills brick red with a flash and dust burst. Bar 3: the message rises word by word, optional team photo in a stamped frame. Bar 4: signature "— the crew (and robots) at Redbrick", glint, exit.
- **Scene "Lights-Out Cell"**. Archetype: **place / diorama**, because Redbrick really is a place, the factory floor, and the product is that floor running by itself (the last kit used a process machine). A cutaway brick factory bay at night: a 5-axis CNC with a glowing window and spinning spindle, a robot arm loading stock, an AGV cart shuttling parcels to a loading dock, an andon light tower, conveyor, sawtooth roof skylights and a "REDBRICK" wall sign. Loops: spindle spins, sparks, AGV drives, conveyor rollers, andon blinks, skylight stars. Moment: a finished part pops out of the CNC with a flash. Variants: night / day shift / dusk / blueprint / sage.
