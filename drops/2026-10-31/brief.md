# OpenAssets Launch — brief

**Use case.** OpenAssets (arnabchatterjee20k.github.io/openassets) is launching: a free, editable asset library where Claude generates a use-case kit every day (icons, a 3D hero, a banner, video templates, an isometric scene) as parametric source code. The site renders every preview live in the browser, and every download (SVG, PNG, GLB, MP4) is rendered on demand from the visitor's own edits. Nothing is pre-rendered. Kits so far: Weather App Launch (29 Oct) and openfeed Launch (30 Oct); this is Kit 03. The team needs library UI icons, a 3D landing hero, the README header banner, a launch countdown video, a promo video and an animated scene.

**Idea.** Print on demand. Each asset is a plate that isn't inked until you press the button, and your edits are the ink. It's a riso zine shop where the run is always one copy, printed for you. Fluoro pink and blue overprint on warm paper, with shouting condensed headlines.

**Reference.** 90s/2010s risograph zine culture (Hato Press, Colorama, Tan & Loose Press): two-ink misregistered overprint, multiply blends, halftone suns and crop/registration marks, crossed with Swiss sports-poster type (huge condensed caps, tight leading, tiny mono captions).

**Palette "Risograph" (light).** `#F4EFE6` paper (bg) · `#EAE2D3` newsprint (surface, panels) · `#1F2A6B` riso navy ink (ink: all small text, outlines) · `#FF48B0` fluoro pink (accent: hero shapes, big type) · `#0078BF` riso blue (accent2: second plate) · `#FFE800` riso yellow (pop: one dot, one sticker, the "live" light). Variants borrow Neo-brutalist, Memphis, Bauhaus, Acid and Candy.

**Type "Poster shout".** Anton for hero words (caps, leading 0.86–0.9, set huge and cropped). Familjen Grotesk for copy and subheads. JetBrains Mono for labels, file formats, params and chips (uppercase, tracking 0.2–0.3em).

**Shared motif.** The **misregistered overprint**: every hero shape and hero word is printed twice, a pink plate and a blue plate offset a few px and multiplied, snapping into registration on the hit. Plus the **registration mark** (⊕ crosshair in a circle) and a **kit tag** ("KIT 03 · 31 OCT 2026"). Every asset has the overprint and at least one mark.

**Assets.**
- **Icons** (library UI): Package (Kits), SlidersHorizontal (Edit params), DownloadSimple (Download), Stack (Layers), Palette (Palette), FilmStrip (Render video), Sparkle (Daily drop), CodeBlock (Source code). Solid black default; regular / bold / thin / duotone / riso tile / sticker variants.
- **3D "Daily Kit Box"** (landing hero): a chunky rounded kit box in fluoro pink with a yellow tape band, flaps open, and three assets riding out of it: a blue icon tile with a sparkle, a yellow mini cube (the 3D kind) and a pink play tile (video). Idle: the assets bob out of the box in a stagger, the flaps breathe and the sparkle spins. Hover: the assets rise higher and fan out. Click: they pop up with a spin and the box squashes. Calm/lively mood; clay / glossy / metal / glass.
- **Banner "README Header"** (1200×630 first, then all sizes): "OPENASSETS" in Anton, overprinted pink over blue, with the tagline "Free, editable assets. A new kit every day." and a mono URL. The hero is a fanned stack of riso-printed asset cards (icon tile, 3D cube, video frame, scene) carrying SVG / PNG / GLB / MP4 chips, a mini params panel with sliders, registration marks and a yellow "KIT 03" sticker. The strip drops the panel and body copy; square/portrait stacks.
- **Video "Launch Countdown"** (countdown, 1080×1920, 10 s, 120 BPM): bar 1: paper feeds in, the eyebrow "Kit 03 drops in" and a registration mark. Beats 4–13: 5·4·3·2·1, each numeral printed on two beats as pink and blue plates sliding in from opposite sides and snapping into registration on the beat, with a halftone sun pulsing behind. Bar 4 (the hit): a flash and ink splat, "OPENASSETS" slams in and "is live." rises. Bar 5: date, CTA pill and URL with dust and a glint, then the exit. An optional logo sits in the end card.
- **Video "Edit Anything Promo"** (promo, 1920×1080, 10 s): bar 1: "FREE ASSETS." slams in overprinted, then "EDIT ANYTHING." Bar 2: an editor mock in which a slider drags, swatches tap and the preview icon recolors live (your screenshot can replace the preview). Bar 3: SVG · PNG · GLB · MP4 chips stamp in on beats under "DOWNLOAD EVERYTHING.", with the caption "rendered in your browser, on demand". Bar 4: "A NEW KIT EVERY DAY" with three kit cards dealt in on beats (Weather App · openfeed · OpenAssets). Bar 5: end card with the name, URL CTA and exit.
- **Scene "Live Editor Stage"**. Archetype: **interface as stage**, because the product *is* the editor (your edits render the asset live and the download is printed on demand), and the last kit already used a process machine. A giant isometric editor slab sits on riso paper under a halftone sun: a params rail where slider knobs glide and three swatch blocks pop in turn; a central canvas plinth where a big extruded sparkle icon recolors in time with the swatches and slowly turns; and a giant cursor that travels from slider to swatch to the DOWNLOAD key. The moment: the cursor presses DOWNLOAD, with a flash and shock ring, and four file tiles (SVG, PNG, GLB, MP4) spring out in arcs onto an outbox tray. Supporting detail: a code panel scrolling source lines, a "rendering…" progress bar, layer chips, registration marks and a kit tag. Variants: default / neo-brutalist / memphis / night (acid) / bauhaus.
