# Ringside Parallel Dialer Kit: brief

**Use case.** Ringside is a power and parallel dialer for SDR teams. Reps open a HubSpot view or a Salesforce report and Ringside dials four leads at once. It drops voicemails, connects the first human to pick up, and logs every call, note and callback to the right record. The team needs in-app icons, a doodle pack for the landing page, a 3D desk-phone hero, three feature cards, a launch banner, a "how it works" tech film, a year-end customer greeting and a landing-page scene.

**Idea.** A sales floor run like a sneaker-drop counter: loud, heavy Japanese-gothic headlines on warm clay paper, with rings rippling out of every line. Four lanes dial at once, three go quiet and **one lights up**. That single connection is the hit in every asset.

**Reference.** 1970s Italian telephone ads (SIP posters, Olivetti print) crossed with Tokyo streetwear drop flyers: flat terracotta fields, offset hard ink shadows, riso grain and tiny techno labels.

**Palette "Terracotta" (light).** `#F1E4D3` clay paper (bg) · `#E5CDB2` fired clay (surface, cards, lanes) · `#3B1F14` burnt umber (ink, all small text, hard shadows) · `#C8553D` terracotta (accent: the connected line, headline shapes, CTA) · `#4F6D58` sage (accent2: dialing, "logged", calm states) · `#F2A541` saffron (pop: the ring flash, one dot or one word). Variants borrow Mid-century, Matcha, Cherry Cola (dark) and Desert Dusk.

**Type "Streetwear".** Dela Gothic One for hero words (huge, tight, sometimes cropped); Familjen Grotesk for copy and subheads; Chakra Petch uppercase, wide-tracked for labels, lane numbers, timers and data chips.

**Motif.** *Four lanes, one ring.* Four parallel bars or lines (L1–L4) with concentric ring ripples coming off the one that connects, finished with an offset umber hard shadow.

**Assets.**
- **Icons** (dialer UI): Dial Out, Call List, Voicemail Drop, Callback, Call Notes, Headset, Warm Transfer, Disposition. Phosphor glyphs, solid black by default; regular/bold/thin/duotone/tile/stamp variants.
- **Stickers** (landing-page doodles): Loop Arrow, Scribble Circle, Ring Sparkles (marks); Ringing Handset, Talk Bubble ("..." typing dots), Callback Clock. Clay, sage and saffron crayon.
- **3D hero "Desk Dialer"**: a chunky rounded desk phone, handset on the cradle, and four line keys on the front. Idle: the keys light in a rolling L1→L4 sweep and one stays lit (connected), with a gentle bob. Hover: the sweep speeds up. Click: the handset hops off the cradle and the ring LED flashes. Calm/rush mood. Clay, glossy, metal and glass materials.
- **3D cards**: *Parallel Dialer* (a handset with a glowing ring band), *Voicemail Drop* (a cassette with a glowing tape window whose reels spin), *Callback Queue* (an alarm clock with a glowing face ring; the hammer rattles on click).
- **Banner "Parallel Dialer Launch"**: headline "Dial 4. Talk to 1." on the left. The hero on the right is four call lanes (Ringing / Voicemail dropped / No answer / CONNECTED) with ring ripples and a "Logged to HubSpot" chip. Stacks on square; the strip drops body copy.
- **Video "How Parallel Dialing Works"** (tech, 1920×1080, 10 s, 60 fps, 120 BPM). Bar 1: eyebrow, then "4 LINES." slams in on the downbeat and "1 CONVERSATION." follows on beat 3. Bar 2: four lanes slide in and dial (timers tick, rings pulse on beats); lanes 1, 2 and 4 drop out one by one. Bar 3 downbeat: lane 3 CONNECTS with a saffron flash and shockwave, and the caller card grows. Bar 4: a CRM log card stamps in ("Logged · callback Tue 10:30") and stats count up. Bar 5: logo/screenshot, CTA pill on a beat, then exit.
- **Video "Ring In 2027"** (greeting, 1080×1080, 8 s). Beat 1: a handset rings (wiggle and ripples). Bar 1 downbeat: "RING IN" sweeps in. Bar 2 downbeat: "2027" slams in huge with a flash and confetti cannons. Then the message words and the signature ("The Ringside team"), an optional team photo in a stamp frame, a second ring at bar 3 and a soft exit.
- **Scene "The Switchboard"**: process machine. Ringside takes a CRM list, dials it in parallel and files the results, so the scene is a clay switchboard. Lead cards feed from a filing-cabinet CRM on the left onto four lanes. Each lane has a desk phone that rings (ripples, a jiggling handset). One lane's lamp goes saffron, the CONNECTED moment, and a stamped log card slides back into the cabinet. Cables sway, a clock ticks, a plant and a mug add life. 6 s loop; day/dusk/night/sage variants.
