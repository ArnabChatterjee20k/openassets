# Postnook Agent Inbox Kit — brief

**Use case.** Postnook gives every AI agent (and the humans who supervise it) a real email inbox from one API call: `POST /v1/inboxes` returns `support-bot@postnook.email`, inbound mail fires a webhook, agents reply in-thread, and anything risky waits in a human review tray. The team needs in-app icons, a doodle pack for the landing page and docs, a 3D hero for the homepage, three feature cards (Inbox API, Inbound Webhooks, Human Review), a launch banner, a promo film, a customer-quote video and an animated landing-page scene.

**Idea.** Email for machines, told like an old village post office: pigeonholes, envelopes, postmarks and handwritten notes, all in tea-house greens. The warmth says "humans are still in the loop"; the crisp grotesk and tidy addresses say "this is an API".

**Reference.** Mid-century British GPO stationery and airmail ephemera (wavy cancellation marks, perforated stamps, rubber postmarks) crossed with the cosy indie-bookshop illustration of Penguin's 2010s clothbound covers.

**Palette "Matcha" (light).** `#EEF1E6` rice paper (bg) · `#DCE3CC` sage card (surface) · `#1E2A1A` pine ink (text, outlines) · `#6A8D3A` matcha (accent: envelopes, CTA, large type) · `#C9A227` brass/wax gold (accent2: seals, stamps) · `#E85D3F` postbox red (pop: one dot, an unread badge, a "new" flag). Variants borrow Terracotta, Nordic Frost, Mid-century, Forest Moss (dark) and Risograph.

**Type "Bookish".** Young Serif for hero words (chunky, warm); Familjen Grotesk for copy, addresses and UI; Caveat for handwritten notes, signatures and margin scribbles ("← your agent", "approved!").

**Shared motif: the postmark.** A ring date-stamp (`POSTNOOK · 26 NOV`) with three wavy cancellation lines, plus perforated stamp edges. It appears as the banner badge, the promo's hit, the quote video's sign-off, the scene's sorting desk and the stamp sticker.

**Assets.**
- **Icons (8, in-app UI):** inbox tray, agent (robot), send, address (@), webhook, attachment, human review (user-check), thread. Solid black default; regular / bold / thin / duotone / tile / letterpress variants.
- **Stickers (6):** loop arrow, scribble circle, sparkles (marks); envelope with a wax seal, perforated postage stamp, and "Nib" — an original envelope mascot with a little antenna.
- **3D hero "Agent Envelope":** a soft envelope with a gold wax seal and a letter peeking out; the letter rises on a breathing loop, the seal glows, a red unread dot blinks; hover opens the flap further, click pops the letter up. Materials clay / glossy / paper / metal / glass; calm / busy mood.
- **3D cards:** *Inbox API* (a glossy mailbox with a glowing flag), *Inbound Webhooks* (a desk bell with a glowing ring), *Human Review* (a rubber stamp with a glowing face).
- **Banner "Agent Inbox Launch":** headline "Every agent deserves an inbox." beside a hero inbox card: three messages (human, bot, review) with avatars, an unread dot, a Caveat note and a postmark badge. Side-by-side wide, stacked square, inbox-only on the strip.
- **Video "Inbox Promo" (promo, 1920×1080, 10 s @ 120 BPM, 5 bars):** bar 1: the code line `POST /v1/inboxes` types itself on a sage card; beat 4 → hit: the card flips into an envelope; the postmark thumps on the downbeat of bar 2 and the address `support-bot@postnook.email` sweeps in. Bar 3: three messages slide into an inbox list on beats (human, bot reply, "needs review" with a red dot) with a Caveat note. Bar 4: offer "3,000 emails / month free" and the CTA pill pop on a beat. Bar 5: hold with drift, a glint, then exit. Optional product screenshot replaces the inbox list.
- **Video "Customer Quote" (quote, 1080×1080, 8 s, 4 bars):** bar 1: an envelope slides up, flap opens; bar 2 downbeat: the letter rises out and the quote writes in word by word in Young Serif; beat 3 a Caveat underline scribbles under the key phrase; bar 3: photo (optional, falls back to initials), name, role and company; bar 4 downbeat: the postmark stamps the corner, then a soft hold.
- **Scene "Postnook Exchange":** archetype **network / system**, because Postnook connects many parties (people, agents, apps) through addressable inboxes; recent kits used process machines twice. A central floating platform holds a wooden pigeonhole wall (one labelled box per agent) and a sorting desk with a postmark press; three satellite platforms (a human's desk with a lamp and steaming tea, a bot rack with a blinking robot, an app server with a webhook dish) are joined by dashed paths along which envelopes travel. Loops: envelopes travel, the robot blinks, tea steams, lamp flickers, dish spins, clouds drift. Moment: a letter drops into a pigeonhole and the red flag pops with a sparkle. 6 s loop; day / dusk / night / riso variants.
