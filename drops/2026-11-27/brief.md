# Loupe Inspection Kit — brief

**Use case.** Loupe is a fictional AI visual-inspection station for precision hardware shops (aerospace fasteners, medical gears, EV bearings). It photographs every part on the line and flags burrs, pits and scratches under 100 μm before they reach assembly. The team needs in-app icons, a doodle pack for the landing page, a 3D hero, feature cards, a launch banner, a trade-show booth invite, a thank-you film for pilot customers and a living hero scene.

**Idea.** Precision made friendly: the cold world of micrometres redrawn as soft, rounded, lilac-tinted product art. Parts look like toys under a jeweller's loupe, a pink ring circles the one tiny flaw, and teal ticks mean "ship it".

**Reference.** 1960s Japanese camera and instrument catalogues (soft studio light, objects floating on pastel sweeps) crossed with Ladislav Sutnar's friendly industrial catalogue graphics: callout lines, measured dimensions, numbered tags.

**Palette "Lilac Haze" (light, unchanged).** `#E9E3F5` lilac (bg) · `#D6CCEC` lavender panels (surface) · `#231942` aubergine ink (text, line work) · `#5E4AE3` loupe violet (accent: lens rings, scan beams, hero type) · `#F49CBB` defect pink (accent2: the flagged-defect ring, rejects) · `#2EC4B6` pass teal (pop: tiny doses, ticks and "PASS"). Variants borrow Nordic Frost, Blueprint, Mid-century, Ink & Gold and Candy.

**Type "Friendly app".** Fredoka (600) for hero words, Outfit for copy and data, Caveat for handwritten callouts ("38 μm burr!", signatures).

**Shared motif: the loupe ring.** A violet circle with tick marks and a crosshair that magnifies part of an object, with one pink circle around a single tiny defect and a Caveat callout on a leader line. Every asset has exactly one "flag" moment and one teal pass mark.

**Assets.**
- Icons (app UI): 8 Phosphor glyphs: Scan, Crosshair, Microscope, Gear, Hex Nut, Pass Seal, Defect Flag, Tolerance Ruler. Solid black by default, with regular/bold/thin/duotone/tile/brutal variants.
- Stickers: Defect Circle (scribble ring), Callout Arrow and Pass Sparkles (marks); Hex Nut and Hand Loupe (objects); Pip, an original round camera-eye inspector bot (mascot).
- 3D hero "Inspected Gear": a glossy precision spur gear with a floating loupe lens that sweeps across its teeth. A pink defect pip blinks and the lens ring glows. Hover speeds up the sweep; a click snaps the loupe to the defect and flashes. Calm/lively mood; clay/glossy/metal/glass.
- 3D cards: "Line Camera" (a camera with a glowing lens), "Tolerance Studio" (a dial indicator with a glowing needle), "Fastener QA" (a hex nut with a glowing serial ring). Their default names were picked so the seeded light gives orange, magenta and cyan, not three greens.
- Banner "Pilot Launch": the headline "Catch 40 μm flaws before assembly." on the left; on the right, a gear inside a big loupe ring with a pink defect callout and stat chips (99.7% first-pass yield, 0.4 s per part). Stacks on square and drops the body copy on the strip.
- Scene "Inspection Line": a **process machine**, because Loupe turns an unsorted stream of parts into sorted pass/reject. (The previous kit also used a machine, but sorting on a conveyor is the clearest picture of inspection.) Parts ride a conveyor under a camera gantry and a violet scan fan sweeps each one. A flagged gear gets a pink flash and drops into a reject bin while good parts go into a tray marked with teal ticks. Status light, monitor graph and spinning rollers. 6 s loop.
- Video "Booth Invite" (event, 1920×1080, 8 s, 120 BPM): bar 1: a loupe ring draws on and scans across a floating gear, and a pink defect ring pops on beat 3. Bar 2 downbeat: "See it catch a 38 μm burr live." sweeps in. Bar 3: the event card (Midwest Precision Expo · Booth 4127 · Mar 9–11, 2027 · Detroit) slides in with chips landing on beats. Bar 4: CTA pill and glint, then exit. Optional booth photo in a rounded frame.
- Video "Pilot Thank-You" (thank-you, 1080×1920, 8 s): a counter rolls up to "1,204,880 parts inspected" while the gear turns. On bar 2, "Thank you, Ridgeline Machining" sweeps in and confetti of teal ticks and lilac nuts arcs up and drifts down. Then a handwritten note and signature in Caveat; bar 4 is the sign-off. Optional team photo in a loupe-ring frame.
