# Pike Ad Agent Kit: brief

**Use case.** Pike (pike.ai) is an AI agent that runs one campaign across 32 ad platforms (Meta, Google, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Reddit, Pinterest, Snap, Amazon, CTV…) from a single canvas: it drafts the creatives, launches them, watches every channel around the clock and moves budget to whatever is biting. It's for two- to five-person growth teams at DTC and B2B brands with no media buyer per channel. They need dashboard icons, a landing-page hero, three feature cards, a launch banner, a release-notes film, a holiday greeting for customers and an animated hero scene.

**Idea.** Ice fishing. A Nordic angler doesn't sit by one hole: they drill thirty, set a *tip-up* over each, and watch for the little flag that springs up when something bites. Pike is that angler for ads: lines in 32 platforms, and an orange flag pops wherever a conversion lands. Calm frozen blues, then one hot flag. The type is a protest placard in the snow: Climate Crisis shouting over quiet grotesk copy.

**Reference.** 1950s–60s Scandinavian winter-sport posters (Oslo 1952, Swedish ski-resort lithographs: flat snowfields, big skies, one red figure) crossed with activist placard typography.

**Palette "Nordic Frost".** `#E9EEF1` snowfield (bg) · `#D3DDE3` lake ice (surface) · `#17242E` deep-winter ink (text, outlines) · `#2F6F8F` fjord blue (accent: shapes, big display type) · `#A7C4D4` frost (accent2: ice, secondary shapes) · `#E4572E` tip-up orange (pop: only the flag / the conversion). Variants borrow Blueprint, Forest Moss, Swiss, Mid-century and Ink & Gold.

**Type "Activist".** Climate Crisis (`climate`) for one or two hero words, huge and tight; Familjen Grotesk (`familjen`) for copy and subheads; Space Mono (`spacemono`) uppercase and wide-tracked for labels, platform names and data.

**Shared motif.** The tip-up flag: a small orange triangle pennant on a thin staff. It is the one hot element in every asset (a popped flag on the 3D hero, flags over ice holes in the banner and scene, the flag that springs up on the beat in both videos). Ice holes, drilled rings in frost blue, are the secondary motif.

**Assets.**
- **Icons** (dashboard UI): Campaigns (megaphone), Audiences (target), Budget (coins), Performance (chart up), Platforms (plugs), Conversions (pennant flag), Agent (sparkle), Freeze Spend (cold thermometer; the fill snowflake muddied at 24 px). Solid black default; frost tile, fjord duotone, brutal and outline variants.
- **3D hero "Pike Tip-Up"** (landing page / App Store): a glossy white cross-frame tip-up standing over a round ice hole with a spool and a spring staff. Rest pose: flag sprung (something bit). Idle: the flag flutters, the line tugs, the spool turns, the hole's water ripples; hover tugs harder; click snaps the staff down and springs it back up. Calm / biting mood; clay, glossy, metal, glass.
- **Card "Audience Finder"**: a white compass with a glowing needle that swings and settles. **Card "Night Shift"** (24/7 optimisation): a white lantern with a glowing core that breathes and flickers. **Card "Connectors"**: a white plug with a glowing ring that pulses.
- **Banner "Launch: 32 Platforms"**: headline "FISH EVERY FEED." in Climate Crisis, copy "One agent. 32 ad platforms. Budget moves to whatever's biting." Hero visual: an isometric-ish field of ice holes with platform labels, three flags popped, glass data chips (ROAS, CPA). Wide → side by side; strip drops body; square stacks.
- **Video "Release Notes"** (tech, 16:9, 10 s @ 60 fps, 120 bpm): bar 1: version chip + "PIKE 2.4" sweeps in, frost grid drifts. Bar 2: feature title "Cross-platform pacing" + a channel table where spend bars rebalance live (or the user's screenshot in a frame). Bar 3 downbeat: a flag springs up on the winning row, +ROAS number counts up, two more bullet points. Bar 4: CTA "Read the changelog → pike.ai/changelog", glint, fade. Bar 5 half: hold + exit.
- **Video "Holiday Greeting"** (greeting, 1:1, 8 s): snow falls on a frozen lake; beat 2 the ice-hole ring draws; beat 3 the tip-up flag springs up with a shock ring and the greeting "GOD JUL" lands in Climate Crisis; then "Happy holidays, Northwind team" and a message; optional team photo sits in the ice hole; signature, second smaller flag pop at bar 3, exit.
- **Scene "Frozen Lake Hero"** (landing hero, animated SVG, 6 s loop). Archetype: **network / system** as a cutaway diorama, because Pike's job is one operator tending many connected channels: a frozen lake with a warm fishing hut (the agent) in the middle, lines running under the ice to 16 holes, each with a tip-up and a platform tag. Lying flags stay frost-grey; orange only marks sprung ones. Loops: hut windows flicker, chimney smoke, antenna pings, water ripples, pulses travel the lines, a fish patrols the cutaway, a skier crosses the far ridge, snow falls. Moment: the TikTok flag springs up with a ring burst, window flash and a "+1 conversion" chip. Variants: day, dusk, polar night (aurora), blueprint, mid-century.
