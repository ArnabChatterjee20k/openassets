# Ad Agent Flight Kit (Towline): brief

**Use case.** Towline (towline.ai) is an AI agent that takes one campaign brief and runs it across 34 ad platforms (Meta, Google, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit, Snap, Pinterest, LinkedIn, CTV…). It builds the creatives, launches them, moves budget toward winners overnight and reports back. It is for in-house marketers at DTC and travel brands who run too many channels with too few people. The team needs dashboard UI icons, a 3D hero for the landing page, a launch banner, a 9:16 promo story for their launch-week offer, a 1:1 customer-quote post, three feature cards and an animated hero scene.

**Idea.** A banner-towing plane at golden hour: one little prop plane carries your message over every beach at once. Towline is the pilot; your brief is the banner. Everything is warm, airborne and a bit glamorous: a holiday postcard, not a dashboard.

**Reference.** 1970s airline travel posters (Pan Am, Braniff "End of the Plain Plane", David Klein) crossed with striped 70s sunset discs. Flat sculpted shapes, big serif headlines, ticket-stub labels.

**Palette "Sunset Sorbet" (light).** `#FFEDE1` peach sky (bg) · `#FFD3C2` sorbet haze (surface: cards, clouds, sand) · `#3D0E2A` plum ink (all small text, outlines, hard shadows) · `#FF4F6D` watermelon (accent: the sun, the tow banner, CTAs) · `#7B2CBF` dusk violet (accent2: the upper sky, the plane, data) · `#FFB627` marigold (pop: the sun's core, one spark or stamp per asset). Variants borrow Desert Dusk (dark), Citrus Pop, Mid-century, Candy and Lilac Haze.

**Type "Gallery".** Gloock for heroes (sculpted heavy serif, tight -0.03em, often mixed case), Syne 700/800 for copy and CTAs, Space Mono uppercase wide-tracked (0.28em) for eyebrows, boarding-pass labels, data chips.

**Shared motif.** **The tow banner + striped sun.** A long pennant banner on a tow line, with a ripple in its tail, carries the headline or platform names. Behind it sits a 70s sun disc sliced by horizontal bands. Labels are styled like boarding-pass fields (`FLIGHT TL-034 · GATE ALL`).

**Assets.**
- **Icons (8, dashboard UI):** launch (PaperPlaneTilt), campaigns (FlagBanner), agent (Sparkle), platforms (SquaresFour), budget split (ChartPieSlice), pacing (Gauge), reach (Eye), conversions (Funnel). Solid black default; regular/bold/thin/duotone/tile (plum tile, watermelon glyph)/brutal variants.
- **3D icon "Tow Plane":** chunky toy prop plane trailing a waving banner on a tow line. Idle: propeller spins, banner ripples, wing light blinks; hover speeds the prop and stretches the ripple; click does a quick barrel roll. Materials clay/glossy/metal/glass; calm/gusty mood.
- **3D cards:** "Flight Plan" (compass, glowing needle swings), "Ad Studio" (instant camera, lens ring glows, flash on click), "Spend Pacing" (hourglass, glowing sand trickles).
- **Banner "Now Boarding":** left: eyebrow `NOW BOARDING · 34 PLATFORMS`, Gloock headline "Brief it once. Fly it everywhere.", Syne body, CTA "Start a flight". Right: a striped sunset sun with the plane towing a banner of platform names, two boarding-pass chips ("$12.4k moved to winners overnight", "CPA −31%"). Strip drops body + one chip; square stacks the sky above the copy.
- **Video "Launch Week Offer"** (promo, 9:16, 8 s @ 60 fps, 120 BPM). Bar 1: sky gradient wipes up, sun rises in stripes, eyebrow "LAUNCH WEEK". Beat 5 (bar 2 downbeat): the plane crosses with the banner "0% FEES"; the headline "60 days, on us." sweeps in. Bar 3: price strike "$149/mo → $0", promo code ticket "TOWLINE60" pops on the beat with a stamp. Bar 4: CTA pill "Start free · towline.ai", glint, expiry line, fade. Optional product screenshot in a tilted card.
- **Video "Customer Quote"** (quote, 1:1, 9 s). Beat 1: huge watermelon quote mark lands with a stamp ring. Bars 1–2: quote words rise in Gloock, the key phrase highlighted with a marigold marker swipe. Bar 3: stat ticket counts "−38% CPA"; avatar (image) + name + role + company slide in on a boarding-pass strip. Hold: sun stripes drift, dust; fade.
- **Scene "Coastline Campaign"** (archetype **vehicle / journey**: the agent carries one brief out to every platform the way a tow plane carries a banner along the whole beach. Yesterday's kit used network/system). An isometric beach at sunset: sea with lapping waves, a sand strip, a boardwalk lined with six platform billboards (story phone, feed square, search bar, TV, video player, pin board), umbrellas, lifeguard tower, sailboat, gulls. Loops: plane with banner crosses, gulls flap, waves lap, buoy bobs, sailboat drifts, sun shimmers. Moment: as the plane passes, the billboards flip to the new creative in a wave, each with a marigold sparkle. Variants: golden hour / dusk / midday / night / citrus.
