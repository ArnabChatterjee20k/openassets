# Ardor Romance Kit: brief

**Use case.** Ardor is an AI romance game for women: pick a world (a Regency ballroom, a vampire court, a rooftop in Neo-Seoul), choose a love interest and talk to them. They remember everything, and every choice changes the story. Ardor is launching its Valentine's world, *The Midnight Court*. The team needs in-app icons, a landing-page hero object, feature cards, a launch banner, a story video for socials, a lower-third for their dev-diary and creator interviews, and an animated hero illustration.

**Idea.** A pulp romance paperback read under the covers at 1 a.m. Cherry-cola dark, lit by a phone screen and one red-hot heart. The covers are confident and editorial, never pink and coy.

**Reference.** 1950s–60s pulp romance paperback covers (big serif title, a tagline in the corner, a price flash) crossed with the typography of a modern art-gallery poster. Valentine lithography supplies the wax seals and scalloped edges.

**Palette "Cherry Cola" (dark).** `#2A0A10` cola (bg) · `#3F1019` velvet booth (surface) · `#FFE8E0` blush paper (ink) · `#E3173E` cherry red (accent, the signature: hearts, seals and CTAs) · `#F5C6AA` peach skin-tone (accent2: secondary light, chat bubbles) · `#F7B32B` gold foil (pop, tiny doses: one sparkle, a gem, a price). Variants borrow Desert Dusk, Ink & Gold, Candy, Sunset Sorbet and Lilac Haze.

**Type "Gallery".** Gloock (heavy sculpted serif) for hero words, set huge with tight tracking and allowed to crop. Syne for copy. Space Mono, uppercase and wide-tracked, for chapter numbers, eyebrows and data ("CH. 07 · AFFECTION 82%").

**Shared motif.** The **wax seal heart**: a scalloped cherry-red seal stamped with a heart, used as the brand mark and the "hit" in every asset (a stamp in videos, a badge on the banner, a glowing seal on cards). A second motif is the **chat bubble**, because the product is a conversation.

**Assets.**
- **Icons (in-app UI):** Affection (Heart), Chat (ChatTeardropDots), Chapters (BookOpenText), Persona (MaskHappy), Wardrobe (CoatHanger), Gems (Diamond), Choices (Signpost), Worlds (Planet). Solid black by default, with cherry tile, blush duotone and brutal variants.
- **Stickers:** Loop Arrow, Scribble Circle and Sparkle Trio (marks), plus Love Letter (envelope with a heart seal), Long-Stem Rose and Heart Bubble (a chat bubble with a heart).
- **3D hero "Heart Chat Bubble":** a glossy, heart-shaped speech bubble with three typing dots that bounce in a wave. Idle: it breathes and the dots type; hover makes it type faster and blush; click gives a heartbeat double-pump and flash. Has a calm/smitten mood.
- **3D cards:** *Story Worlds* is a crystal orb on a pedestal with a glowing core. *Chapter Pass* is a closed book with a glowing bookmark ribbon. *Gem Shop* is a ring with a glowing cut gem.
- **Banner "New World Launch":** a paperback-cover headline on the left ("Fall for someone new tonight."). On the right, a phone-less chat thread with the love interest (Lucien), an affection meter and two choice chips, with a wax seal badge saying "NEW WORLD". It stacks on square and drops the chat to one bubble on the strip.
- **Scene "Midnight Nightstand"** (object still life, because Ardor is a nightly ritual of reading and chatting in bed): a nightstand at 1 a.m. with a phone propped on paperbacks and chat bubbles popping up, a lamp that flickers, a candle flame, steam from tea, a rose in a vase dropping a petal, and a moon through the window with drifting clouds. The moment is a heart burst over the phone when he replies.
- **Video "Launch: The Midnight Court"** (9:16, 8 s, 120 BPM). Bar 1: a velvet dark field, chat bubbles type in ("You came back." / "I always do."). Bar 2 downbeat: the wax seal stamps down with a flash, shockwave and camera shake, and the world title sweeps in huge. Bar 3: the love-interest card slides up (image param, or a monogram fallback) and the date "FEB 14" lands on a beat. Bar 4: a glint, then the CTA pill pops and everything fades.
- **Video "Lower Third: Creator Card"** (16:9, 6 s, transparent-friendly dark scrim). Beat 1: a cherry bar wipes in from the left. Beat 2: the seal pops and the name sweeps in. Beat 3: the role and @handle type in. Hold with a breathing glow and a slow sheen. Exit on bar 3 with the bar wiping out. Has a photo param in a circular frame.
