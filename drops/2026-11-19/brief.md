# Crossfade Ad Agent Kit — brief

**Use case.** Crossfade is an AI ad agent for lean growth teams: you write one brief in chat, and it builds, launches and rebalances campaigns across 31 ad platforms (Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Reddit, Snap, CTV…), "mixing" budget toward whatever performs, like a DJ riding faders. The team needs dashboard icons, a landing-page hero, feature cards, a launch banner, a milestone story and a product intro film, plus an animated hero scene.

**Idea.** The agent as the DJ of your ad spend. Every platform is a channel strip on a mixing desk; the agent rides the faders at golden hour while the room warms up. Warm desert dusk, neon tubes, LED level meters climbing.

**Reference.** Late-70s disco record sleeves and Route 66 neon motel signs at sunset (multi-line tube lettering), crossed with Dieter Rams-era audio hardware (white bodies, one glowing LED).

**Palette "Desert Dusk" (dark).** `#2B1B2E` plum night (bg) · `#45293F` dune shadow (surface) · `#FFE9D6` warm sand (ink) · `#FF8E5E` sunset coral (accent: faders, meters, CTAs) · `#E5487A` neon rose (accent2: glows, second light) · `#FFD27F` sodium gold (pop: one LED, the peak, the gold record). Variants borrow Ultraviolet Club, Acid, Ink & Gold, Sunset Sorbet and Cherry Cola.

**Type "Disco night".** Monoton for the hero words only, at large sizes (neon tube lettering). Syne 800 for headlines and copy. Space Mono, uppercase and wide-tracked, for labels, platform names and data.

**Shared motif.** The channel strip: vertical fader slots with a cap and a stack of LED level segments (coral → rose → one gold peak). It appears as the banner's console, the hero object, the video intro's light wall, the scene's mixing desk and the milestone's level meters.

**Assets.**
- **Icons** (dashboard nav): Faders (channel mixer), Robot (agent), Broadcast (launch everywhere), Coins (budget), UsersThree (audiences), ImagesSquare (creatives), TrendUp (performance), Waveform (live signals). Solid black by default; regular / bold / thin / duotone / neon-tile / brutal variants.
- **3D hero "Crossfade Mixer"** (landing page / app store): a compact white mixing console with four channel faders, LED meters and a glowing crossfader. Idle: faders ride a slow groove and meters bounce; hover makes the meters jump; a click slams the crossfader across with a flash. Materials clay / glossy / metal / glass; calm / live mood.
- **Cards** (feature list): "Auto-Mix Budgets" (a rotary knob with a glowing LED ring that sweeps), "Creative Booth" (a studio mic whose grille band glows; the agent writes and renders creatives), "Live Monitor" (headphones with glowing ear rings; 24/7 performance watch).
- **Banner "31 Platforms Launch"**: Monoton "CROSSFADE" wordmark, Syne headline "One brief. 31 platforms. Mixed live.", and a hero mixing console on the right: channel strips labelled GOOGLE / META / TIKTOK / LINKEDIN / REDDIT… with fader caps at different levels and LED meters, a sun disc rising behind it. Stacks on square; strip mode drops the body.
- **Video "Gold Record"** (milestone, 1080×1920 story, 8 s, 120 BPM). Bar 1: a black vinyl spins in from below, the tonearm drops on beat 3, grooves light up. Bar 2 downbeat: the record turns gold with a flash and shockwave; "$50M" in Monoton counts up on beats with the label "AD SPEND MIXED". Bar 3: thank-you line word by word, the optional image sits in the record label, LED meters bounce along the bottom. Bar 4: glint, CTA pill, eased fade.
- **Video "Meet Crossfade"** (intro, 1920×1080, 8 s). Bar 1: a wall of 31 channel strips; faders push up in a left-to-right wave on beats, meters fill. Bar 2 downbeat: the strips dim, the Monoton wordmark lights tube by tube with a flicker, rose glow blooms. Bar 3: tagline words in, three platform chips pop on beats ("31 platforms · 1 brief · 0 spreadsheets"). Bar 4: glint, CTA, fade. Optional logo image beside the wordmark.
- **Scene "Golden Hour Mix"** (landing hero, 6 s loop). Archetype: **process machine**, because Crossfade takes one brief and mixes it out into many platform channels (the recent Firebrand scene was interface-as-stage). An isometric DJ deck on a sandstone platform in the desert at dusk: a brief card feeds into the console's slot, faders ride, a turntable spins, patch cables carry pulses to a stack of speaker cabinets labelled with platforms whose cones thump, a disco ball turns over it all, mesas and a setting sun behind. Moment: the drop: every meter peaks, the disco ball throws a flash of sparkles and a "+31 LIVE" tag pops. Variants: night, daylight, ultraviolet, acid, ink & gold.

## Append: second run

More of the product's surface, same palette, type and channel-strip motif.

- **Icons** (the rest of the dashboard nav): ChatCircleText (brief), ArrowsSplit (A/B tests), Crosshair (targeting), CalendarCheck (schedule), SealCheck (approvals), BellRinging (alerts), ChartPieSlice (reports), PuzzlePiece (integrations). Same schema and variant block as the first eight.
- **3D icon "Launch Boombox"** (app-store / launch-day hero): a chunky white boombox with glowing coral speaker rings, a rose EQ window, carry handle and antenna. Idle: cones thump on every beat, EQ bounces, the gold ON AIR light blinks twice; hover turns it up; a click drops the bass. Clay / glossy / metal / glass; calm / party mood.
- **Cards**: "Weekly Mixtape" (a cassette whose reels turn behind a glowing tape window: the Monday report), "Audience Radar" (a satellite dish that pans the sky, its receiver pulsing and pinging: lookalike discovery), "Brand Guard" (a heater shield with a glowing gem and a scan line sweeping its face: brand-safety approvals).
- **Banner "ROAS Case Study"**: a customer story post. Monoton "+38%" stat, "ROAS in 30 days", Syne headline, and a mixdown console as the hero: each platform's LED meter shows its new share of spend over a dashed ghost of the old one, with a gold "+22 PTS" tag on the winner and a "CPA −22%" chip. Strip mode keeps stat + headline + compact console; square stacks the console on top.
- **Video "On Air Lower Third"** (lower-third, 1920×1080, 6 s, 120 BPM). Beat 1: a channel strip rises and its fader pushes up. Beat 2: the name plate wipes out of the strip with a flash and the name sweeps in. Beat 3: title words. Beat 4: the LIVE tag pops. The meter pulses through the hold, a glint passes the name, then the plate pulls back into the strip. Optional headshot and background still; backdrop toggle for overlay use.
- **Video "Crossfade 2.0 Launch"** (launch, 1920×1080, 12 s). Bars 1–2: Deck A lists the old way (tabs, spreadsheets, 3 a.m. bid edits) on beats and strikes them out, while Deck B waits with an unlit "2.0". On the last beat a giant crossfader slams A → B. Bar 3 downbeat: flash, shockwave and streaks; the wordmark lights tube by tube and "2.0" lands. Bar 4: the title glides into the left column, the product screen (optional screenshot, otherwise a live mixer UI) rises on the right. Bars 4–5: headline, three feature chips and a launch-date tag on beats. Bar 6: CTA, glint, fade.
- **Scene "Night Shift Desk"** (empty-state / "while you sleep" hero, 6 s loop). Archetype: **object still life**, because the other side of Crossfade is that it works when you don't. The first scene showed the machine; this one shows the user's desk at 2:47 a.m. A cutaway room shows the chair pushed back, steam rising from the mug, headphones set down and the lamp on, while the laptop keeps riding the faders. A desert night shows through the window, an ALWAYS ON neon sign glows and a gold record hangs on the wall. Moment: the phone buzzes, a notification pops ("Agent moved $1.2k → TikTok", ROAS 4.2×), the meters peak and a shooting star crosses the window. Variants: dawn, ultraviolet, ink & gold, acid.
