# openfeed Launch — brief

**Use case.** openfeed, a personalised reading feed (github.com/ArnabChatterjee20k/openfeed), is launching: a calm daily archive of everything worth reading from the sources you keep. Under the hood: **Scout** collects sources, **FeedX** gathers, scores and distills the day, **domdistill** reduces pages to their readable substance. The team needs reading-UI icons, a 3D landing hero, a launch banner, a how-it-works tech video, an announcement video and an animated isometric scene.

**Idea.** A morning paper left on a tea-house step. Instead of a firehose, one page per day that rises like the sun and stays put. Slow, warm, collected; juicy 70s headlines on calm green paper.

**Reference.** 70s "supergraphics" (stacked rainbow stripe arcs on Barbara Stauffacher Solomon / Alexander Girard interiors) and 70s Penguin/Pelican paperback covers, set in a Japanese tea-house palette.

**Palette "Matcha" (light).** `#EEF1E6` rice paper (bg) · `#DCE3CC` tatami (surface) · `#1E2A1A` sencha ink (ink, all small text) · `#6A8D3A` matcha (accent) · `#C9A227` honey (accent2) · `#E85D3F` persimmon (pop: one dot, one word, the "today" marker). Variants borrow Terracotta, Mid-century, Nordic Frost, Forest Moss and Risograph.

**Type "70s sunshine".** Shrikhand for hero words (juicy italic, set big and tight); Fraunces for copy and article titles (warm, bookish — it *is* the reading face); Outfit for labels, data and chips (uppercase, wide tracking).

**Shared motif.** The **sunrise stripe**: three nested arcs — matcha, honey, persimmon — rising behind "today's page". Plus the **day tab**: a rounded date tab ("Fri 30 Oct") on cards. Every asset has one of each.

**Assets.**
- **Icons** (reader UI): Newspaper (Today's edition), BookOpenText (Reader), BookmarkSimple (Keep), RssSimple (Sources), CalendarDots (Daily archive), Archive (Archive), Funnel (Distill), Binoculars (Scout). Solid black default; regular/bold/thin/duotone/tile/sticker variants.
- **3D "Daily Page Stack"** (landing hero): a soft stack of rounded paper pages, today's page on top with a matcha header, text lines and a persimmon ribbon; a 70s triple-stripe arch rises behind. Idle: the top page corner peeks, the ribbon sways, the stripes pulse in sequence. Hover lifts the page higher; click flips it open and the arch pops. Calm/lively mood.
- **Banner "Launch Day"** (OG / X / LinkedIn / YouTube / square): Shrikhand headline "Your reading, kept daily." left; right a fanned stack of day cards (real-looking article titles + source chips + scores) over sunrise stripes; stat chip "214 read → 9 kept". Strip drops body copy; square stacks.
- **Video "How openfeed Works"** (tech, 16:9, 10 s, 120 BPM): bar 1 — sources (RSS chips) fly in to Scout; bar 2 — FeedX scores the cards, low scores dim and drop on beats; bar 3 — domdistill: a cluttered page (nav, ads, popups) peels away to clean Fraunces text; bar 4 — the day's page lands with sunrise stripes, "openfeed" in Shrikhand + repo CTA; bar 5 hold with glint, exit. Optional screenshot image replaces the page body.
- **Video "openfeed Is Open"** (announcement, 9:16, 8 s): beat 1 sunrise stripes sweep up from the bottom; beat 3 "openfeed" pops in juicy and wobbles to rest; "is open." in Fraunces italic; date stamp; three day cards deal in on beats; CTA pill on bar 3; dust + breathing sun throughout. Optional screenshot sits inside the top card.
- **Scene "openfeed Reading Room"** (landing hero / empty state): floating isometric garden island — tea-house reading pavilion with a sunrise-stripe sign, Scout's lookout tower with a turning telescope, domdistill's copper still dripping drops, a mailbox where paper planes (sources) land, an archive shed of day drawers, lamps, bushes, stepping stones. Moment: the day's paper drops at the door with a pop and the sun brightens. Day / dusk / night / autumn / riso variants.
