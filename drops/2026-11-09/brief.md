# Switchboard Ad Agent Kit (Patchwell): brief

**Use case.** Patchwell (patchwell.ai) is an AI agent that takes one campaign brief and runs it across 33 ad platforms (Meta, Google, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Snap, Amazon, CTV…): it writes the variants, launches them, rebalances budget overnight and leaves a memo on your desk at 8 a.m. It's for small marketing teams at home, furniture and lifestyle brands who can't hire a media buyer per channel. They need dashboard icons, a landing-page hero, three feature cards, a launch banner, a "new lines connected" announcement film, a countdown story for their launch day (Tue 18 Nov 2026) and an animated landing-page scene.

**Idea.** The agent is a 1950s switchboard operator. Your brief is a call; Patchwell patches it through to every line at once. Warm walnut, cream enamel, bakelite, little amber jack lamps lighting up one by one, cords swooping in catenary curves. Calm, friendly and competent, the opposite of a neon AI dashboard.

**Reference.** Mid-century Bell System and Herman Miller print ads, Charley Harper's flat geometric illustration and Alexander Girard's textile color. Chunky friendly serif headlines on cream stock, handwritten operator notes in the margins.

**Palette "Mid-century" (light).** `#F4E9D8` cream stock (bg) · `#E6D3B3` oatmeal / walnut-light (surface: panels, cards) · `#2E2A24` espresso ink (all small text, outlines, hard offset shadows) · `#D96C3D` burnt orange (accent: the patch cord, hero shapes, CTAs) · `#2C7873` teal enamel (accent2: the console, secondary cords, data) · `#E9B44C` mustard (pop: the lit jack lamp, one spark per asset). Variants borrow Terracotta, Bauhaus, Nordic Frost, Cherry Cola (a night shift) and Matcha.

**Type "Bookish".** Young Serif (single weight, set large, tracking −0.02em) for heroes; Familjen Grotesk for copy and chips; Caveat for operator notes, eyebrows and signatures ("— your operator").

**Motif.** The patch cord: a single orange cord with a brass plug, drawn as a soft catenary swoop, ending in a jack whose lamp lights mustard. Every asset has one cord and one lit lamp. Starburst sparks (atomic-age asterisks) mark the "moment".

**Assets.**
- Icons (dashboard UI): Phosphor glyphs: operator (Headset), patch-in (PlugsConnected), brief (Notepad), campaigns (Megaphone), budget (PiggyBank), audiences (Target), rebalance (PhoneTransfer), morning memo (EnvelopeSimple). Solid black default; regular/bold/thin/duotone/tile/brutal variants in kit colors.
- 3D hero "Hotline Phone" (landing page): a chunky rotary telephone in glossy burnt-orange bakelite with a cream dial, coiled cord and a mustard "line lit" lamp. Idle: lamp blinks, dial does a small spin and returns, handset gives a tiny ring-jiggle. Hover: lamp brightens, dial spins faster. Click: ring shake + lamp flash. `material` clay/glossy/metal/glass, calm/ringing mood.
- Cards: "Patch Bay" (platform connections → a single jack plug with a glowing tip ring), "Night Shift" (overnight budget rebalancing → a twin-bell alarm clock with a glowing face ring), "Morning Memo" (8 a.m. report → a closed hardback book with a glowing ribbon bookmark).
- Banner "33 Lines Open" (launch, all sizes): headline left; hero visual right: a walnut jack panel with 3×N jacks labelled by platform, lamps lit mustard, one orange cord swooping from a brass plug into it, a handwritten operator note. Stacks on square; strip drops body copy.
- Scene "Switchboard Desk" (landing hero, 8 s loop). Archetype: **interface as stage**: the switchboard *is* Patchwell's UI, so the product is a mid-century console on a walnut desk in a cream study: an 11×3 jack panel with flickering lamps, cords that sway, a brief card sliding into the in-tray, the phone dial turning, a starburst clock whose minute hand runs the loop, a framed bird print, a window with drifting clouds and a paper plane, steaming coffee, a plant, a desk lamp. Moment: all 33 lamps ripple on together when the brief is patched through. (Last kit used a seabed network; this is a still-life interface instead.) Variants: day, dusk, night shift, terracotta, matcha.
- Video "New Lines Connected" (announcement, 16:9, 8 s, 120 BPM): bar 1: camera close on the switchboard, jacks pop in, the orange cord draws in from off-frame and the plug seats on the downbeat of bar 2 (ring + starburst + shake, lamps cascade on). The camera pans out; title "Now patched into TikTok Shop" sweeps in with the eyebrow note "New lines · Nov 2026", then three platform chips pop on beats. Bar 3: body line, CTA pill, glint. Bar 4: lamps ripple, hold, exit. Optional product shot slides into a framed "screen" on the right.
- Video "Lines Open Countdown" (countdown, 9:16, 10 s, 120 BPM): a rotary dial fills the upper half. Bar 1 builds (dial pops in, "ring ring…"); from bar 2, each number 5→1 is "dialled" every two beats (the finger wheel turns the hole to the stop exactly on the beat, then springs back) with a big Young Serif digit in the centre card, an orange ring pulse and the 33 bezel lamps filling up; at 7 s the wheel spins a full turn, a sunburst opens and "Lines are open." lands with a starburst and the date/time line; the host photo (optional) sits in a round cameo; CTA "Set a reminder" pops; hold with dust and a lamp ripple.
