# Inkrun Ad Agent Kit: brief

**Use case.** Inkrun is an AI ad agent for small growth teams. You hand it one brief and it builds, launches and re-budgets campaigns across 31 ad platforms (Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Reddit, Snap, Pinterest, CTV…), resizing every creative for each placement and moving spend to whatever works. The team needs dashboard icons, a landing-page hero, feature cards, a launch banner, a "how it works" film, a holiday greeting, and an animated hero scene.

**Idea.** The agent as a night-shift gig-poster print shop. One design gets pulled through 31 screens, and each platform gets its own print run, numbered by hand. The inks are fluorescent (UV violet, hot pink and a single yellow), printed on deep aubergine stock under blacklight.

**Reference.** 1990s screen-printed rock gig posters (Frank Kozik, Jay Ryan / The Bird Machine): two-ink overprint, slight misregistration, halftone dots and pencilled edition numbers.

**Palette "Ultraviolet Club" (dark).** `#12001F` aubergine night (bg) · `#24003D` poster stock (surface) · `#FBEFFF` paper white (ink) · `#B026FF` UV violet ink (accent: shapes, rollers, CTAs) · `#FF2E88` hot-pink ink (accent2: the second pass, overprints) · `#FFD600` highlighter yellow (pop: one edition number, one spark). Variants borrow Risograph, Lava, Acid, Ink & Gold and Mid-century.

**Type "Warm craft".** Fraunces 900 (soft, wonky old-style) for heroes, set tight with one italic word. Outfit for product copy. Caveat for hand-written edition numbers, notes and signatures ("12/31", "— the Inkrun crew").

**Shared motif.** *The misregistered pull:* every hero shape or word is printed twice, violet with a hot-pink pass offset a few pixels (multiplied or screen-blended), and carries a hand-written Caveat edition mark (`31/31`). Halftone dots fill the shadows.

**Assets.**
- **Icons** (dashboard nav): Robot (agent), ChatTeardropText (brief), ShareNetwork (31 platforms), PiggyBank (budget), Target (audiences), PaintBrushBroad (creatives), ChartLineUp (performance), Lightning (autopilot rules). Solid black by default; regular / bold / thin / duotone / ink-tile / misprint variants.
- **3D hero "Ink Brayer"** (landing page / app store): a chunky hand roller with a white frame, wooden handle and glossy violet-inked roller resting on a small ink slab, with a fresh stripe of ink printed behind it. Idle: the roller rocks forward and back, spinning, and the stripe breathes; hover makes it roll faster; a click squashes it and pops a pink ink blob. Clay / glossy / metal / glass; calm / rush mood.
- **Cards** (feature list): "Budget Pacer" (a stopwatch with a glowing bezel and a sweeping hand), "Creative Studio" (a compact camera with a glowing lens ring; resizes every creative), "Audience Finder" (a magnifying glass whose lens glows and pans).
- **Banner "Print Run Launch"**: headline "One brief. *Thirty-one* print runs." with a hero fan of five misregistered mini-posters in real ad formats (9:16 TikTok, 4:5 Meta, 1:1 LinkedIn, 16:9 YouTube, 2:3 Pinterest), each tagged with a Caveat edition number, and a violet squeegee stripe behind. Stacks on square; the strip drops the body copy.
- **Video "How Inkrun Works"** (tech explainer, 1920×1080, 10 s, 120 BPM, 5 bars). Bar 1: the brief card types itself (optional image = your product shot inside the card). Bar 2 downbeat: a squeegee sweeps across and the brief prints into a 31-tile platform grid, in pink first and then violet, which snaps into register on beat 2. Bar 3: three stat chips count up on beats (ROAS 4.2×, CPA −38%, 31 live). Bar 4: wordmark with overprint and CTA pill. Bar 5: hold with drifting halftone, then fade.
- **Video "Holiday Print"** (greeting, 1080×1920 story, 8 s). Bar 1: blank stock, then violet ink pulls "Happy" in a squeegee sweep. Bar 2: pink pulls "holidays" misregistered, then snaps into register on the downbeat with a yellow star. Bar 3: the message line word by word, the optional team photo as a duotone print, Caveat signature. Bar 4: edition stamp "2026 · 1/1", glint, eased fade.
- **Scene "Night Press"** (landing hero, 6 s loop). Archetype: **process machine**, because Inkrun turns one brief into many platform-sized prints (Crossfade's second scene was a still life). An isometric carousel screen-printing press in a blacklit print shop: the brief sheet slides in on a feed table, the four-arm carousel rotates, a squeegee pulls across the active screen, and finished posters clip onto a drying line labelled with platforms while a conveyor dryer glows. Moment: a fresh print drops onto the stack with a pink flash and a "31/31" tag pops. Variants: dusk, daylight, risograph, lava.
