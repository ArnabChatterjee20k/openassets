# SaaS Product Launch — brief

**Use case.** Strobe (strobe.dev), a feature-flag and progressive-delivery platform, ships v1.0 on Tue 18 Nov 2026. Flags, gradual rollouts, audience segments and a one-click kill switch, so teams can ship on a Friday. Pricing: Free for 3 environments, Team $29/mo. The team needs dashboard icons, a 3D landing hero, the launch banner, a customer-quote video, a launch-night event video and an animated landing scene.

**Idea.** Shipping is a party, not a war room. Every release is a switch flipped on the beat. Black room, acid-green light, magenta and cyan lasers, an 8-bit attract screen showing the rollout percentage.

**Reference.** 1988–92 acid-house rave flyers and The Designers Republic's Warp sleeves (hard black fields, fluoro ink, tiny technical captions), crossed with arcade attract screens: chunky isometric Nabla titles and Press Start pixel labels.

**Palette "Acid" (dark).** `#0B0B0B` club black (bg) · `#1A1A1A` booth (surface, cards) · `#F0FFE0` lime-white (ink, all small text) · `#C6FF00` acid green (accent: the ON state, CTAs, the hit) · `#FF00E5` magenta laser (accent2: second light, highlights) · `#00F0FF` cyan (pop: one dot, the live LED). Variants borrow Ultraviolet Club, Lava, Neo-brutalist, Deep Sea and Risograph.

**Type "Arcade".** Nabla for hero words only (huge, isometric colour layers recoloured to the palette with `@font-palette-values`). Rubik Mono One for copy and sub-heads (caps, tight). Press Start 2P for labels, data and chips (integer sizes, wide tracking).

**Shared motif.** The **flag pill**: a rounded toggle in the ON state (acid track, ink knob) that appears in every asset, plus **strobe bars** (horizontal scanline stripes that flash on the hit) and a **rollout readout** ("ROLLOUT 25%" in pixel type).

**Assets.**
- **Icons** (Strobe dashboard): ToggleRight (Feature Flags), Percent (Rollout), UsersThree (Segments), ArrowCounterClockwise (Rollback), Power (Kill Switch), Flask (Experiments), WebhooksLogo (Webhooks), RocketLaunch (Releases). Solid black default; regular / bold / thin / duotone / acid tile / sticker variants.
- **3D "Feature Toggle"** (landing hero): a chunky rounded toggle switch, knob in the ON position on a glowing acid track, set in a bevelled booth-black base with a cyan status LED and a pixel "ON" plate. Idle: the LED blinks, the track glow breathes, the knob gives a little nudge. Hover: glow intensifies, knob leans. Click: the knob flicks off and back on with a flash ring. Calm / lively mood; clay / glossy / metal / glass.
- **Banner "Strobe 1.0 Launch"** (all BANNER_SIZES): Nabla headline "SHIP ON FRIDAY", eyebrow "STROBE 1.0 · OUT NOW", body copy, CTA "Start free → strobe.dev". Hero: a flag console card ("new-checkout", toggle ON, rollout bar at 25%, segment chips, live conversion sparkline +12.4%) with laser beams and strobe bars behind. Strip drops body + chips; square stacks the console above copy.
- **Video "Customer Quote"** (quote, 1080×1080, 8 s, 120 BPM). Bar 1: strobe-bar flash, a giant Nabla quote mark pops on beat 1; quote words rise in on beats 2–4. Bar 2: the key phrase ("40 times a day") gets an acid marker swipe on the downbeat; laser sweeps. Bar 3: headshot ring (or initials) + name, role, company slide in; a stat chip "2 → 40 deploys/day" pops. Bar 4: logo pill "Strobe · strobe.dev", glint, exit.
- **Video "Launch Night"** (event, 1080×1920, 10 s, 120 BPM). Bar 1: black, strobe flashes on each beat, laser fan opens, "STROBE 1.0 PRESENTS". Bar 2: "LAUNCH NIGHT" slams in Nabla with shockwave + shake. Bar 3: the line-up types in on beats (18:00 keynote, 18:40 live rollout, 19:30 DJ set). Bar 4: date/venue ticket stub drops in, optional photo in a halftone frame. Bar 5: RSVP pill pops, glint, lasers fade, exit.
- **Scene "Rollout Network"**. Archetype: **network / system**, because Strobe connects one flag console to many environments and users (the last two kits used a process machine and an interface stage). A central console platform with a giant isometric toggle and a rollout gauge, joined by glowing cables to three environment platforms (DEV, STAGING, PROD) carrying server racks with blinking LEDs; flag packets travel along the cables; a crowd of user blocks on PROD. Moment: the giant toggle flips, a flash rings out and PROD's users light up acid green. Variants: default / ultraviolet / lava / daylight (neo-brutalist) / deep sea.
