# Plotwell Ad Agent Kit: brief

**Use case.** Plotwell (plotwell.ai) is an AI agent that runs ads on 33 ad platforms (Meta, Google, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Reddit, Pinterest, Snap, Amazon, CTV and more) from one canvas. You give it a goal and a budget. It drafts the media plan, generates the creatives, launches them everywhere and rebalances spend hourly. It's for small growth teams at DTC and B2B brands that can't hire a media buyer for every channel. They need dashboard icons, a landing-page hero, three feature cards, a launch banner, a product announcement film, a launch-livestream countdown story and an animated hero scene.

**Idea.** The campaign as a construction drawing. Plotwell doesn't guess. It *drafts*: every campaign starts as a cyanotype plan sheet with a title block, dimension lines that carry budgets ("$4,200 / day") and compass arcs that link the brief to each platform. Then a pen plotter draws it live. White hairlines on deep cyan, with one yellow highlighter stroke marking whatever just shipped.

**Reference.** 1900s cyanotype architectural blueprints and Anna Atkins' cyanotype plates, crossed with the "Art school" posters of the 1960s Ulm/Basel school, where technical drawing became graphic art. Also HP pen plotters from the 1980s.

**Palette "Blueprint"** (dark). `#0F3B8C` cyanotype (bg) · `#134AA8` sheet panel (surface) · `#EAF2FF` drafting white (ink, body copy) · `#FFFFFF` hairline white (accent: linework, big display type) · `#7FB2FF` pale cyan (accent2: grid, secondary lines, construction marks) · `#FFDD00` highlighter yellow (pop: one swipe, one dot, the "live" moment). Variants borrow Whiteprint (diazo paper `#F1EEE4` + blue ink `#173F9A`), Bauhaus, Ink & Gold, Swiss and Desert Dusk.

**Type "Art school".** Syne ExtraBold (`syne`) for hero words: wide, huge, cropped when it helps, tracking −0.04em. Bricolage Grotesque (`bricolage`) for copy. Major Mono Display (`majormono`) in lowercase for title-block labels, dimension text, platform names and data. Its odd caps mix reads as drafting lettering.

**Shared motif.** The **dimension line**: a white hairline with tick ends and a mono label riding on it, used for budgets, spans and timings. A **yellow highlighter swipe** marks the one thing that matters in each asset. Secondary motifs: the title block (bottom-right sheet stamp), compass arcs and registration crosshairs.

**Assets.**
- **Icons** (dashboard UI): Media Plan (blueprint), Agent (compass tool), Campaigns (megaphone), Targeting (crosshair), Budget (coins), Pacing (gauge), Platforms (squares four), Creatives (images). Solid black by default; regular / bold / thin / duotone / cyanotype tile / whiteprint outline variants.
- **3D hero "Drafting Compass"**: a glossy white drafting compass standing on its needle, with a hinge knuckle, a knurled handle and a yellow lead that glows. Rest pose: legs open and balanced. Idle: the compass pivots a quarter turn and back, as if scribing an arc, while the lead pulses. The legs breathe open and closed. Hover opens the legs wider and brightens the lead; a click spins a full arc with a flash. Calm / drafting mood; clay, glossy, metal, glass.
- **Card "Media Plan"**: a white rolled blueprint tube with a glowing band that breathes. **Card "Budget Balance"** (hourly rebalancing): a white spirit level whose glowing bubble drifts and re-centres. **Card "Creative Proofs"**: a white loupe whose glowing lens ring pulses.
- **Banner "Launch: 33 Platforms"**: headline "Draw the plan. We'll run it." in Syne. Body: "One agent drafts, launches and rebalances your ads on 33 platforms." Hero visual: a plan sheet. A brief node at the centre links through compass arcs to platform nodes, dimension lines carry budgets, a title block sits in the corner and the top channel gets a yellow highlight. Wide: side by side. Strip: drop the body copy. Square: stack.
- **Video "Plotwell 3 Announcement"** (announcement, 16:9, 10 s, 120 bpm). Bar 1: grid draws on, crosshair lands, mono eyebrow "announcing · nov 18 2026" types. Bar 2 downbeat: "Plotwell 3" sweeps in wide while a dimension line extends under it. Bar 3: the plan sheet plots. Brief node, arcs out to 6 platform tags on beats, and the user's screenshot (optional) as a framed "detail A". Bar 4 downbeat: the highlighter swipes "33 platforms", the counter ticks up and the headline lands. Bar 5: CTA "plotwell.ai/3", title block stamps, glint, exit.
- **Video "Launch Countdown"** (countdown, 9:16, 9 s). Bar 1: title "Live demo" with a date chip, compass arc rings. Beats 4–9: 3 · 2 · 1, each digit drawn huge in Syne with a compass arc sweeping a full turn around it, dimension ticks counting. Bar 3 downbeat: the highlighter flash shows "We're live", the host photo (optional) appears in a round porthole and the CTA "Join at plotwell.ai/live" with a bounce. Hold with drifting grid, exit.
- **Scene "Plotter Desk"** (landing hero, animated SVG, 6 s loop). Archetype: **process machine**, because Plotwell turns a brief into a drawn plan and then into live ads. The scene shows an isometric drafting table with a pen plotter gantry that slides back and forth while the pen draws routes on the blueprint sheet. A brief card feeds in on the left; finished ad cards pop out on the right and slide down a chute into six platform trays with labels. There's a lamp with a flickering bulb, a spinning reel of paper, a pinned plan on the wall and a coffee cup with steam. The moment: a yellow "LIVE" stamp slams onto the sheet with a ring flash. Variants: night shift, whiteprint, sepia studio, bauhaus.

## Append: more of the Plotwell kit (same day)

- **Icons** (8 more, same schema + variants block): Conversions (funnel), Reports (chart line up), Schedule (calendar dots), Split Test (arrows split), Brand Safety (shield check), Integrations (puzzle piece), Alerts (bell ringing), Ad Specs (ruler).
- **3D icon "Agent Bot"**: the Plotwell agent as a mascot for onboarding, chat and empty states. A rounded white robot head with a cyanotype visor (faint grid hairlines and a scan line), glowing pill eyes, dial ears and a compass-needle antenna with a yellow status tip. Idle: glances, blinks (one double blink), antenna wobble and flicker, ears tick round. Hover makes the eyes grow and the tip brighten; a click gives a happy squint and an antenna flash. Calm / busy mood; clay, glossy, metal, glass.
- **Card "Brand Guard"** (brand-safety checks): a white heater shield with a glowing faceted gem that sends out a scan ring. **Card "Audience Finder"**: a white map pin with a glowing core that taps down and drops ground pulse rings. **Card "One-Click Launch"**: a white rocket with a glowing porthole, a light-coloured band and a flickering exhaust plume.
- **Banner "Case Study: CPA Cut"** (customer results, the proof post after the launch): "CPA down 38% in six weeks." with the highlighter on "38%". The hero visual is an *elevation drawing*: weekly CPA columns drawn as hatched architectural sections on the plan sheet, a ground line, a trend line over the column tops, a dimension line carrying "−38%" and a was/now title block. The final week is the yellow column. The strip shows a cropped, wider slice of the sheet. Variants: whiteprint, "ROAS up" (rising columns), bauhaus, ink & gold, desert dusk.
- **Video "Growth Plan Promo"** (promo, 16:9, 8 s, 120 bpm). Bar 1: grid draws, the eyebrow types and "Plan Q4 for less." sweeps in while a dimension line measures it. Bar 2 downbeat: "40% off" slams in with a flash and streaks, the highlighter swipes it on the next beat, and the old price is struck through. Bar 3: the hero slides into the left column and a "detail b · live plan" panel (or the user's screenshot) wipes in with budget bars growing; the feature checklist ticks in on beats and a dimension line carries the deadline. Bar 4: CTA and dashed promo-code chip, glint, blur-out exit.
- **Video "Customer Quote"** (quote, 4:5, 10 s). Bar 1: a compass scribes the two opening quote marks, which fill yellow on beat 4. Bar 2: the quote lands word by word. Bar 3: the highlighter swipes the result as one stroke while a dimension line measures it ("6 weeks · 9 channels"). Bar 4: the customer appears in a compass-ring porthole (photo or initials), with name and role. Bar 5: hold with turning ticks, glint and dust, then exit.
- **Scene "Platform Network"** (landing hero, animated SVG, 6 s loop). Archetype: **network / system**, because Plotwell's promise is one plan connected to many platforms, which the plotter-desk scene (a process machine) doesn't show. A plan hub floats at the centre: a disc where a compass arm scribes the plan, a needle mast and a yellow beacon. Drafting-rail bridges run out to six floating platform tiles, each with a screen glyph, budget bars and its name. Ad cards travel out on every rail and budget coins travel back; arrival rings flash on the tiles. Construction circles turn on the ground plane below, and a paper plane crosses the sheet. The moment: the hourly rebalance, when a highlighter pulse races out from the hub, every budget bar re-levels and the winning tile raises a yellow flag. Variants: night shift, whiteprint, bauhaus, desert dusk.
