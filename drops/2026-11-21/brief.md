# Multiball Ad Agent Kit: brief

**Use case.** Multiball is an AI ad agent for e-commerce and app teams. Connect your accounts once and it writes, launches and re-budgets campaigns on 34 ad platforms at the same time (Google, Meta, TikTok, Amazon, Reddit, Snap, Pinterest, CTV…), moving spend to whatever is scoring. The team needs dashboard icons, a landing-page hero, feature cards, a launch banner, a trial-offer promo, a customer-quote video and an animated hero scene.

**Idea.** In pinball, *multiball* is the moment several balls are in play at once and every bumper lights up. Here the agent is the player: the budget is the ball, the platforms are the bumpers, and ROAS is the high score. The kit looks like a 1986 pinball backglass redrawn by the Memphis Group: cream laminate, candy-pink and teal blocks, confetti squiggles, hard ink outlines and 8-bit score readouts.

**Reference.** Memphis Group (Ettore Sottsass, Nathalie Du Pasquier's laminate patterns) crossed with Bally/Williams pinball playfields and arcade attract screens.

**Palette "Memphis" (light).** `#FFF6E9` cream laminate (bg) · `#FFE3D3` peach (surface: cards, playfield) · `#1A1A2E` ink (outlines, small text, offset shadows) · `#FF6F91` bubblegum pink (accent: bumpers, big shapes, CTAs) · `#00B3A4` teal (accent2: lanes, second shapes) · `#FFC93C` sunflower (pop: the ball's glint, one score, one star). Variants borrow Neo-brutalist, Risograph, Citrus Pop, Candy and Ultraviolet Club (a night "arcade after dark" variant).

**Type "Arcade".** Nabla (colour isometric 3D letters) only for huge hero words ("MULTIBALL", "50% OFF", "5.4×"). Rubik Mono One for short copy, in caps and kept short. Press Start 2P for labels, scores and chips, at integer sizes ("HI-SCORE", "P1", "ROAS 4.8×").

**Shared motif.** *The score readout and the squiggle.* Every asset has one bouncing ball (pink or chrome with a sunflower glint), a Memphis confetti layer (squiggles, triangles, dots and hatched circles in pink, teal and ink), hard 6–10 px ink offset shadows on cards, and an 8-bit score chip that treats a marketing number as a high score.

**Assets.**
- **Icons** (dashboard nav): Joystick (autopilot), CirclesThreePlus (34 platforms), Coins (budget), Crosshair (audiences), MegaphoneSimple (campaigns), Trophy (winning ads), ChartLineUp (performance), ImagesSquare (creatives). Solid black by default. Variants: regular / bold / thin / duotone / laminate tile / memphis brutal.
- **3D hero "Pop Bumper"** (landing page / app store): a glossy pinball pop bumper (white base, pink skirt, teal cap) with a glowing ring and three balls in orbit, which is the multiball. Idle: the balls orbit and hop, the ring lamps chase, and the cap kicks once per loop. Hover makes the lamps chase faster; a click fires the bumper (squash plus a ring flash). Clay / glossy / metal / glass; calm / tilt mood.
- **Cards** (feature list): "Autopilot" (an arcade joystick with a glowing ball top that rocks), "Budget Caps" (a big arcade token standing on edge, with a glowing inlaid star, spinning slowly), "Audience Match" (a bullseye target with a dart and a glowing centre that pulses).
- **Banner "Platform Launch"**: "34 platforms. *One* player." A hero mini pinball playfield in Memphis laminate: platform bumpers (META, TIKTOK, GOOGLE, AMAZON) with a ball mid-ricochet, a dashed trajectory, confetti squiggles and an 8-bit "HI-SCORE ROAS 4.8×" chip. The copy column is on the left; stacks on square; the strip drops the body copy.
- **Video "Trial Offer Promo"** (promo, 1080×1080, 8 s, 120 BPM, 4 bars). Bar 1: a ball launches from the plunger and ricochets off three platform bumpers on beats, and each hit pops "+1,000". Bar 2 downbeat: TILT-style flash and "50% OFF" slams in in Nabla with confetti squiggles bursting out. Bar 3: "First 3 months · code MULTI50" in chips, one per beat, with the optional product screenshot in a tilted, ink-shadowed laminate frame. Bar 4: the CTA pill pops on beat, an "Ends Dec 2" pixel tag, a glint, then the ending.
- **Video "Customer Quote"** (quote, 1080×1920 story, 10 s, 5 bars). Bar 1: the Memphis frame builds (blocks slide in from the edges). Bar 2: a giant pink quote mark drops on the beat, then the quote comes in word by word in Rubik Mono. Bar 3 downbeat: a HI-SCORE board flips the ROAS from 3.1× to 5.4× with a flash. Bar 4: avatar (optional photo, otherwise initials) plus name and role slide in, then the logo line. Bar 5: hold with drifting confetti, then exit.
- **Scene "Multiball Table"** (landing hero, 6 s loop). Archetype: **interface as stage**, because Multiball's dashboard *is* a pinball table: the bumpers are the ad platforms, the ball is the budget, and the backglass is the ROAS scoreboard (Inkrun used process machine and network). An isometric pinball table on cream laminate with legs, backglass, flippers, lanes, platform-labelled bumpers that flash when hit, two balls travelling on SMIL paths, chasing lane lights, a spinning spinner and a blinking score. Moment: MULTIBALL, where the backglass flashes and a third ball pops out with a confetti burst. Variants: night arcade, risograph, citrus, mint.

## Append run: more assets

- **Icons** (dashboard nav, same schema and variants as the set): Flask (A/B tests), Funnel (conversions), Plug (integrations), MagicWand (AI ad copy), CalendarCheck (schedule), BellRinging (alerts), SlidersHorizontal (bid rules), PresentationChart (reports).
- **3D icon "Flipper"** (app-store / onboarding hero): a glossy cream pinball flipper with a pink rim, a teal pivot cap and a chrome ball. The flipper is the agent's hand that keeps the budget in play. Idle: it flicks and the ball pops up and lands. Hover readies it; a click fires it. Clay / glossy / metal / glass, candy or chrome ball, calm / lively mood. Variants: clay, chrome, after-dark, glass, citrus.
- **Cards** (feature list): "Ad Studio" (a white clapperboard that claps once per loop as its REC lamp flashes, for AI video and image creatives), "Live ROAS" (a dial gauge whose glowing arc and needle sweep up, for live reporting) and "Integrations" (a plug standing prongs-up on a coiled cable, whose status ring blinks "connecting" then locks on as the plug turns slowly in the beams).
- **Banner "High-Score Results"**: a customer case-study post. A giant Nabla "5.4×" stat ("ROAS in 30 days") sits next to a tilted 8-bit HI-SCORE cabinet that ranks platforms by ROAS, with budget bars shifting to the leaders. Riso, citrus (Black Friday recap), candy (CTV) and after-dark variants.
- **Video "Arcade Live Event"** (event, 1920×1080, 8 s, 120 BPM): a coin drops into the slot on beat 1 (INSERT COIN → CREDIT 01), "AD ARCADE LIVE" slams in under chasing marquee bulbs, the speaker slides in on an arcade screen (optional photo), date/time/place chips land one per beat, then a "Save your seat" pill and a ticking countdown.
- **Video "High-Score Thanks"** (thank-you, 1080×1350, 10 s): a high-score board counts up to the milestone and the initials Y-O-U lock in one per beat. Then THANK YOU slams in with confetti, followed by a word-by-word note, an optional team photo (or a flipping milestone token) and a "Continue?" pill.
- **Scene "Creative Factory"** (6 s loop). Archetype: **process machine**, because Multiball turns one product brief into dozens of platform-sized ads and ships them out (the existing scene is interface as stage). Product photos drop into a hopper, a gumball "brain" tumbles, and a press stamps blank cards into square, story and banner ads on a belt. A dispatch tower routes them to META / TIKTOK / GOOGLE tubes, with gears, gauges and a ticking ad counter around it. Moment: a gold star stamps a winning ad, a WINNER! chip pops and confetti bursts.
