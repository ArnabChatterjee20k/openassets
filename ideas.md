# Use-case backlog

Each day's drop is a **kit for a real job**: the assets an actual team would need for it. The daily job takes the first kit here that hasn't been used yet. If the list runs out, Claude proposes a new real-world use case. Add, remove or reorder freely, and prefix an item with `[x]` to skip it.

Format: `- Kit name: who it's for, and what they need`

- SaaS Product Launch: startup shipping v1. Feature icons, 3D hero object, launch banner, launch video, changelog announcement
- E-commerce Flash Sale: online store. Cart/bag/tag/truck icons, 3D gift box, sale banners, countdown promo video
- Fintech App: banking/payments app. Wallet/card/transfer icons, 3D card or coin, trust banner, feature explainer video
- Developer Tool Release: CLI/API product. Terminal/code/git icons, 3D terminal cube, docs hero banner, tech release video
- Webinar & Live Event: B2B marketing. Calendar/mic/users icons, 3D ticket, registration banner, event countdown + speaker lower-third
- Food Delivery App: restaurant ordering. Bowl/bike/clock icons, 3D burger or takeaway bag, promo banner, order-tracking promo video
- Fitness & Health App: workout tracker. Heart/flame/timer icons, 3D dumbbell, challenge banner, milestone celebration video
- Hiring Campaign: company recruiting. Briefcase/handshake/rocket icons, 3D badge, "we're hiring" banner, culture announcement video
- Podcast Launch: new show. Mic/headphones/waveform icons, 3D microphone, episode cover banner, episode intro + audiogram-style video
- Online Course: education creator. Book/certificate/play icons, 3D graduation cap, enrollment banner, course trailer video
- Travel Booking: trip planner app. Plane/map/bed icons, 3D suitcase, destination banner, offer promo video
- Real Estate Listing: agency. House/key/map-pin icons, 3D house, listing banner, open-house invite video
- Customer Support Center: help desk. Chat/ticket/check icons, 3D chat bubble, help-center hero, thank-you video
- Crypto & Web3 Dapp: wallet/dapp. Wallet/link/shield icons, 3D token, mint banner, launch countdown video
- Music Release: artist single drop. Disc/note/play icons, 3D vinyl, pre-save banner, release teaser video
- Nonprofit Fundraiser: charity drive. Heart/hand/globe icons, 3D donation jar, campaign banner, milestone thank-you video
- Mobile Game Launch: indie game. Gamepad/trophy/coin icons, 3D treasure chest, store banner, trailer intro video
- Security Product: cybersecurity SaaS. Shield/lock/key icons, 3D padlock, trust banner, tech feature video
- Wedding Invitation: couples/planners. Ring/heart/calendar icons, 3D ring box, save-the-date banner, invite video
- Restaurant Opening: local business. Chef-hat/fork/star icons, 3D cloche, opening banner, grand-opening announcement video
- AI Assistant Product: AI startup. Sparkle/brain/chat icons, 3D orb, feature banner, product demo intro video
- Newsletter Growth: creator newsletter. Envelope/pen/chart icons, 3D mailbox, subscribe banner, milestone thank-you video
- Holiday Season Campaign: brand seasonal promo. Gift/snowflake/star icons, 3D ornament, holiday sale banner, greeting video
- Productivity App: tasks/calendar app. Check/calendar/timer icons, 3D checklist, onboarding banner, feature announcement video
